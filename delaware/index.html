Currey Farms

Harrington, DE

Grass-fed, grain-finished beef from cattle the family breeds themselves. Cattle spend most of their time grazing pasture, eat hay and forages grown on the farm, and are finished with non-GMO grain the farm grinds itself. No antibiotics, growth hormones, GMOs or steroids (per the farm)

Products: individual cuts priced per lb: steaks (ribeye, NY strip, filet, porterhouse, T-bone, sirloin, flat iron, skirt, flank), roasts, brisket, short ribs, ground and stew meat, organs and soup bones (heart, liver, tongue, oxtail), 10 lb ground beef package ($54) and $400 Freezer Filler package…

Shipping: No shipping shown. Order by the form on the farm's beef page and the farm contacts you to schedule pickup at 712 Flat Iron Rd, Harrington (open Thu and Fri 12-5, Sat 8-12)
