Maple Hill Farm

Hardwick, MA
angus

Angus cattle raised 100% grass-fed on pasture, without antibiotics, hormones or GMOs per the farm

Products: steaks (rib eye, NY sirloin, tenderloin, flat iron, flank, skirt, hanger, chuck, top round), roasts (chuck, eye round, bottom and top round, shoulder, ribeye) and brisket, ground beef, 6 oz patties, stew meat, kabob meat and steak tips…

Shipping: Ships frozen to Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maine, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York and Vermont ($25 for up to 30 lb, rising $25 per additional 30 lb). Also farm pickup at 1278 Prouty Road, Hardwick, and local delivery by ZIP code

No phone number or email shown on the farm's current website; reach the farm through its online contact form. Orders go through the farm's Barn2Door online store; many cuts showed low inventory tonight. Grass-fed and no-antibiotics/hormones claims are the farm's own.
