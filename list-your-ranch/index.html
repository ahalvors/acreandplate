Questions

Do you take a cut of my beef sales?

No. Not on any tier. Buyers pay you directly, and you keep 100% of every sale.

What does "verified" mean?

We check that you raise the cattle and sell beef direct to buyers, and we confirm your contact details and how you sell before your listing goes live. We recheck listings on a regular cycle.

When does Premium Profile launch?

We're opening it to a small group of listed ranches first. Join the waitlist and we'll email you before it opens. Nothing is charged to join the waitlist.

Can I combine Premium Profile and Featured Ranch?

Yes. Premium Profile makes your listing richer. Featured Ranch puts it in front of more buyers. They work on their own or together.
