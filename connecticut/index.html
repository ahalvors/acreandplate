Devon Point Farm

North Stonington, CT

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef, never fed grain, raised on pasture with rotational grazing and round-baled hay in winter; no hormones, vaccines or antibiotics per the farm. The farm also breeds rare American Milking Devon (Ruby Red Devon) cattle as Livestock Conservancy members; the beef breed is not named

Products: individual cuts (tenderloin, ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, London broil, flank, skirt and hanger steaks, steak tips), ground beef and 6 oz burger patties, roasts, brisket, stew meat, short ribs, soup bones and marrow bones…

Shipping: No shipping shown. On-farm pickup by appointment in North Stonington (bulk beef picks up in Woodstock or North Stonington), plus delivery by special arrangement on the farm's routes around New Haven, Hartford, Providence, Worcester and the Boston area

Phone 860-942-6292, devonpointfarm@gmail.com, 54 Jeremy Hill Road (open by appointment only). The farm describes itself as an organic farm, but no organic certification is shown on its site, so it is not listed as certified. Grass-finished and no-hormones/antibiotics/vaccines claims are the farm's own. Bulk prices tonight: half $7.85/lb and quarter $8.05/lb hanging weight.
