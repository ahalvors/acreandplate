Polyface Farm

Swoope, VA

100% grass-fed beef; cattle moved daily on fresh pasture with portable electric fencing (the farm's "salad bar beef"); no vaccines or antibiotics per the farm; not USDA certified organic (the farm describes itself as "Beyond Organic"); breed not named on the site

Products: 100% grass-fed beef, pastured pork, pastured chicken and turkey…

Shipping: Ships frozen via UPS to the contiguous US every week: UPS 1-2 day Ground ($25 delivery fee, free over $175, $50 minimum) or UPS 2-Day Air ($35 fee, $50 minimum); also on-farm store and pickup locations/neighborhood delivery clubs across the region (pickup has no minimum and the lowest prices). Prices vary by delivery method and are shown after creating a free account

Address 43 Pure Meadows Ln, Swoope, VA 24479; phone 540-885-3590 and Wendy@polyfacefarm.com per the site footer. Shipping terms per the site's How to Buy page (Nov 2, 2026). The farm says some products come from nearby partner farms and are labeled as such. Beef prices are not public (account required), so none are listed. No breed or third-party certification claimed.
