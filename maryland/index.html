Roseda Farm (Roseda Black Angus)

Monkton, MD
angus

Black Angus (Aberdeen Angus) bred from bulls selected for marbling, tenderness and fat thickness; the farm references its Wye Line genetics and breeding program and says it tracks cattle from embryo to plate

Products: steaks, roasts, dry-aged burgers and ground beef…

Shipping: Ships frozen on dry ice to the continental US, Alaska and Hawaii (FedEx Overnight or 2-Day; free shipping on orders over $100); farm stores in Monkton and Frederick, MD; weekday walk-up pickup at Old Line Custom Meat Co. in Baltimore; free freezer-beef delivery in the greater Baltimore area

Grass-fed, grain-finished. No growth hormones; antibiotics only when medically necessary, per the farm. Natural, not organic. Cattle are raised with about 45 local partner farms and processed at Roseda's own plants in Baltimore City, and the beef is also sold at Giant Food and other Maryland grocers, so buy direct at shop.roseda.com or the farm stores. Monkton farm store open Tue-Sat; website customer service (410) 800-8035.

Nick's Organic Farm

Adamstown, MD
angus

Black Angus. The farm says the cattle are 100% grass-fed their entire lives and never fed grain, kept on pasture all day, every day and never housed indoors, then fed the farm's hay and baleage after the growing season, plus well water and mineral salts mixed with sea kelp, and are not fed hormones, GMOs, antibiotics, or animal products. The farm says the pastures and the hay are certified organic

Products: whole, half, quarter, and eighth beef shares by hanging weight (frozen; dry-aged; every share cut from split halves so customers get the same cuts), additional lean ground beef (80-85%) and ground-beef patties from a bulk share, hanger steak, organs (liver, heart, tongue, tail, cheeks, kidneys, sweetbreads), marrow and soup bones, suet, and trim fat…

Shipping: No shipping shown. Pickup at the farm, 2733 Buckeystown Pike, Adamstown, MD 21710, or at 8565 Horseshoe Lane, Potomac, MD 20854. Beef is sold by advance order, with order forms sent in May and September

Prices are from the farm's Fall 2026 order form (revised 8/26/26), still linked from the homepage on 2026-11-19: https://nicks.farm/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Beef-Mailing-FALL-2026.pdf. That form's cutoff was October 8, 2026, with pickup on Saturday and Sunday, November 7-8, 2026, so the window had closed by this check. The form says the next order form will be sent in May 2027 for pickup in mid-July 2027. Hanging-weight prices in the form's table: eighth $8.00/lb (about 55-80 lb hanging, 37-50 lb finished, about $440-$640, $50 deposit), quarter $7.50/lb (110-160 lb hanging, 75-100 lb finished, $825-$1,200, $100 deposit), half $7.00/lb (220-320 lb hanging, 150-200 lb finished, $1,540-$2,240, $200 deposit), whole $6.75/lb (410-640 lb hanging, 300-400 lb finished, $2,767.50-$4,320, $400 deposit). The whole-beef checkbox on the same form prints $6.50/lb, which does not match the $6.75/lb table, so both figures are left as printed. Minimum deposit $25. Additional lean ground beef (80-85%) $10.75/lb. Hanger steak $16/lb (limit 1). Marrow bones $5/lb. Other bones $4.50/lb in 3, 5, and 10 lb bags, or $3/lb in a 15 lb bag or 40 lb box. Liver $8 (about 0.7-1.2 lb), heart $7 (about 2 lb, limit 2), tongue $9 (about 2 lb, limit 2), tail $9 (about 1-1.5 lb, limit 1), cheeks $7 (about 1 lb, limit 1), kidneys $5 (about 1 lb), sweetbreads $6 (about 1.5 lb, if available), suet $7 (about 2.5-5 lb). Sausage $13 per 1 lb pack; breakfast links $13.50 per 1 lb pack; a six-pack is $70 and an eight-pack is $95. Jerky is $13 for a 4 oz packet. Orders go by mail, email, or hand delivery to the Potomac porch; the form says phone, text, and fax orders are not accepted. Phone 301-983-2167, fax (301) 983-0290, nicksorganicfarm@comcast.net. The order page lists credit-card deposit hours as Monday-Thursday, 11:30 am-3:30 pm, with a 5% fee on the deposit. The form says the farm is certified organic by the Maryland Department of Agriculture and certified by the Real Organic Project, and that the Cornucopia Institute gave its highest ratings for the farm's beef, poultry, and egg practices. Those are the farm's own claims and were not independently checked. The same form says Maryland has no certified organic beef processor, so once the animal is processed the beef is no longer certified organic; the farm still calls it all natural and 100% grass-fed. The processor is not named. The about page describes an 8-12 year crop rotation; the order form says a 12 year rotation. The printed form uses www.nicksorganicfarm.com. Also sells pastured chicken the farm calls certified organic, eggs ($7/dozen when picking up an order; self-serve eggs otherwise), and turkeys (Thanksgiving 2026 was sold out, with a waitlist).
