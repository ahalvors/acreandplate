Roseda Farm (Roseda Black Angus)

Monkton, MD
angus

Black Angus (Aberdeen Angus) bred from bulls selected for marbling, tenderness and fat thickness; the farm references its Wye Line genetics and breeding program and says it tracks cattle from embryo to plate

Products: steaks, roasts, dry-aged burgers and ground beef…

Shipping: Ships frozen on dry ice to the continental US, Alaska and Hawaii (FedEx Overnight or 2-Day; free shipping on orders over $100); farm stores in Monkton and Frederick, MD; weekday walk-up pickup at Old Line Custom Meat Co. in Baltimore; free freezer-beef delivery in the greater Baltimore area

Grass-fed, grain-finished. No growth hormones; antibiotics only when medically necessary, per the farm. Natural, not organic. Cattle are raised with about 45 local partner farms and processed at Roseda's own plants in Baltimore City, and the beef is also sold at Giant Food and other Maryland grocers, so buy direct at shop.roseda.com or the farm stores. Monkton farm store open Tue-Sat; website customer service (410) 800-8035.
