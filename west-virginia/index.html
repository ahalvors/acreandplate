Triple E Farms

Romney, WV

Breed not named on the farm's public site; cattle are 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, rotated to a fresh paddock daily under management-intensive grazing

Products: steaks (ribeye, NY strip, filet mignon, T-bone, porterhouse, flat iron), roasts, ground beef and ancestral blend (beef and organs)…

Shipping: Ships frozen to the lower 48 states via UPS or FedEx on a weekly schedule ($75 minimum; 1-day ground $19.95, 2-day ground or air $30, free ground shipping over $150); local home delivery in the greater DC area; on-farm store in Romney (full store Thu-Sat) and pickup locations near Romney

No antibiotics or hormones, GMO-free, corn- and soy-free, per the farm. Not certified organic. The farm says it has grown from one farm into a network of small farmers working under the same standards, so not every item is necessarily from the home farm. Product prices are shown only after creating a free account. Contact by phone or the site's contact form; no public email listed.
