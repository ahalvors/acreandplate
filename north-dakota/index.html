Nourished By Nature (Brown's Ranch)

Bismarck, ND

All beef born and raised on the family's own ranch east of Bismarck (no outsourcing from other ranches); 100% grass-fed and grass-finished on a diverse diet of grass and forbs, no grain ever; on pasture year-round; Tier 5 Regenified certified per the farm

Products: steaks (ribeye, NY strip, tenderloin filet, hanger, flat-iron, flank, coulotte, teres major, sirloin), roasts (prime rib, chuck, rump, arm, sirloin tip, tri tip) and brisket, ground beef (several lean ratios) and patties…

Shipping: Home delivery to the lower 48 states, shipped frozen on Mondays for Tuesday-Thursday delivery (SpeeDee in ND, SD, MN, IA, WI and parts of NE and IL; UPS elsewhere); $175 order minimum, $25 delivery fee, free shipping over $199. Weekly pickup at the farm (3752 106th St NE, Bismarck) and Bismarck pickup spots, plus farmers markets

Cattle breed not named on the ranch's website. Prices show only after creating an account. The Regenified and drug-free claims are the ranch's own. Email listed is the farm pickup contact (Jazmin Brown); the site also has a contact form.
