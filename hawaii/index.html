Sugar Hill Farmstead

Pepeekeo, HI

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef raised on the family's own pasture on the Hamakua Coast of Hawaii Island; rotational grazing, no added growth hormones, antibiotics or vaccines per the farm

Products: beef shares (1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and whole, plus low-histamine half and whole shares), beef boxes and bundles (Family Farm Beef Box, Favorites Box, Slow Cooker Sampler, Beef Roast Sampler, ground beef bundles), ground beef and Primal Ground (dry-aged ground beef blended with liver and heart)…

Shipping: Ships frozen by FedEx within Hawaii only (Hawaii Island, Oahu, Maui, Kauai and Molokai); shipping starts at a 20 lb order and beef shares can take up to 2 weeks to ship. Farm pickup and local delivery on Hawaii Island. No mainland shipping

Cattle breed not named on the farm's website. Ships only within Hawaii. Many individual cuts showed sold out tonight; beef shares and boxes were available. The hormone-, antibiotic- and vaccine-free claims are the farm's own.
