Kinderhook Farm

Valatie, NY

100% grass-fed and grass-finished cattle raised exclusively on diverse pasture forages and hay, with rotational grazing on preserved Hudson Valley farmland that was converted from a conventional dairy to pasture-based beef in 2004 per the farm

Products: Quarter Cow package (~75 lb: ribeye, T-bone, NY strip, sirloin, flat iron, Denver, coulotte, flap, flank/skirt, tri-tip and chuck eye steaks, brisket or chuck roast, short ribs, osso buco, rolled roast, kielbasa, hot dogs, burger patties, stew meat, ground beef and soup bones), half and whole beef, custom cut to your cutsheet, grass-fed beef soup bones, suet and beef bone broth…

Shipping: Pickup only at the farm store, 1958 County Route 21, Valatie, NY (open 7 days, 9am-5pm). The farm says bulk boxes cannot be delivered.

Public prices seen 2026-11-14: Quarter Cow $955 (~75 lb, about 8 weeks' prep); half beef $7.25/lb and whole beef $7/lb hanging weight (average half about $2,356) with a $200/$400 deposit; MeatShare $650/year or $350/half year (2026 sign-ups currently show out of stock). Bulk beef is processed at a USDA-inspected facility the farm describes as Animal Welfare Approved, and the farm calls its MeatShare meats Animal Welfare Approved; that is the farm's own claim. Cattle breed is not stated on the farm's site. Also sells grass-fed lamb, pastured pork, chicken and eggs, and runs an on-farm Farm Stay. Call or text the farm store number for orders.
