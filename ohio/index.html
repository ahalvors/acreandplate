Fancy Farm Wife

Raymond, OH
HighlandAngus

Mostly Scottish Highland cattle with some Angus, kept on a grass-only diet; the farm describes its beef as 100% grass-fed, pasture-raised and grass-finished from slow-grown heritage cattle, never from a feedlot

Products: ribeye, strip, T-bone, Delmonico, Denver, sirloin and tenderloin steaks, ground beef, stew meat…

Shipping: Local delivery only: free home delivery in Ohio on orders of $200+, delivered personally by the farmers rather than by UPS/USPS. Farm pickup in Raymond, Ohio (call ahead).

Farm store: The Fancy Farm Wife Mercantile, 22193 Herd Mcilroy Road, Raymond, OH 43067; pickup Mon-Fri 5:30-7 PM and Sun 1-4:30 PM, call ahead. Bulk beef questions: Russ at (937) 209-5177. Public prices seen 2026-11-11 include Highland ribeye $26.50, NY strip $42.25 and Farmer's Choice boxes from $165/month (10 lb); freezer beef is priced by hanging weight with a deposit (quarter share deposit $300) plus about $1.10/lb hanging weight paid to a state-inspected processor. The farm says its beef is Certified Quality Highland Beef (QHB) verified by the American Highland Cattle Association; that is the farm's own claim and has not been independently checked. Also sells Berkshire-cross pork, free-range eggs, farm apparel and livestock hauling. Checked 2026-11-11.
