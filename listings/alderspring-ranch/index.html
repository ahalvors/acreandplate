Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished Black Angus; USDA certified organic and Real Organic Project certified; regeneratively raised on wild mountain pastures; no hormones, no GMOs; antibiotics only for sick animals — those animals never sold as organic

About

Family ranch in Idaho's Pahsimeroi Valley raising certified organic, 100% grass-fed and grass-finished Black Angus on wild mountain pastures and home-ranch forage. Ranch-direct since 2005; ships frozen organic beef nationwide (lower 48).

Note: Family ranch (Glenn & Caryl Elzinga and daughters) in Pahsimeroi Valley, May, ID 83253 (7073 Custer Road). Online store: https://shopalderspring.com/. Email: help@alderspring.com. Phone intentionally not published on ranch site (request callback by email). Also sells organic lamb, pastured poultry/pork, dairy seasonally — directory focus is ranch-direct beef.
