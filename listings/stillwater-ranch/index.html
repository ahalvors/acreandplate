Stillwater Ranch

Manhattan, MT

Genetics

Purebred, registered Murray Grey herd (American Murray Grey Association); 70+ pairs on high elevation grass without creep; converted from commercial Angus to regeneratively grazed Murray Grey grass-finished program in 2015

About

Purebred Murray Grey cattle raised on diverse native grass meadows in Manhattan, Montana. Grass-fed and grass-finished with no grain, creep, molasses, urea, or growing hormones. Rotationally grazed from calving to harvest. Full buy back guarantee. Contact to be put on order list for spring and fall availability.
