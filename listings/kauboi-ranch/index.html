Genetics

Grass-fed and grass-finished Angus and American Wagyu (cross between Japanese Wagyu and American breeds); carefully bred using elite Wagyu-influenced genetics; raised without hormones; animals requiring antibiotics removed from program

About

Fifth-generation Oregon rancher Billy Buchanan continuing a family legacy spanning over 100 years. Grass-fed and grass-finished Angus and American Wagyu raised in Central Oregon's high desert with ranching legacy and modern Wagyu genetics.

Note: Formerly Keystone Natural Beef. Ranch pickup available at 655 SW 7th St., Redmond, OR 97756 (Mon-Fri 10am-5pm). Contact: keystonecattle@outlook.com
