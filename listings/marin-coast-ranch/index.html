Marin Coast Ranch

angus

Genetics

Selectively Angus herd; USDA Certified Grassfed and Certified Natural; 100% grass-fed and grass-finished (no grain); regenerative family ranch

About

Sixth-generation Northern California family ranch in West Marin raising USDA Certified Grassfed, Certified Natural Angus beef on coastal pasture. Cattle are grass-fed and grass-finished with dry-aged, vacuum-sealed cuts. Also sells grass-fed lamb; offers ranch hikes, picnics, and tours.

Note: Address: 900 Cerini Road, Tomales, CA — visits by appointment only. Email: marincoastranch@gmail.com. Spring 2026 beef shares sold out as of 2025-04-25 per ranch site; accepting deposits for Spring 2027 harvest starting 1/1/27. Confirm current retail cut inventory and pricing on their store before ordering.
