Home Place Pastures

Como, MS

Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef; regenerative grazing systems; no antibiotics, ever; no hormones; own USDA-inspected slaughter and processing facility on-farm

About

Fifth-generation family farm in Como, Mississippi, rooted to the land for over 150 years. Transitioned from row crops to regenerative grazing a decade ago. One of the only farms in the country operating its own on-farm USDA slaughter and processing facility. 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef raised in regenerative grazing systems that build soil health. Also runs farm store and restaurant open Tuesday-Saturday.

Note: Address: 1630 Home Place Rd, Como, MS 38619. Farm store open Tuesday-Saturday 8am-5pm, serving lunch 11am-3pm. Also offers processing services to other local farmers. Cattle breed not specified on public site.
