Triple E Farms

Romney, WV

Genetics

Breed not named on the farm's public site; cattle are 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, rotated to a fresh paddock daily under management-intensive grazing

About

Mennonite family farm in the mountains of Romney (Hampshire County), West Virginia, started in 2015 and now run by the founder, his wife Esta and their three kids. Beef cattle are 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, moved to fresh pasture usually every day with management-intensive grazing and a small amount of organic minerals; the farm describes its approach as regenerative. Also sells grass-fed raw dairy, pastured chicken, turkey, eggs and forest-raised pork.

Note: No antibiotics or hormones, GMO-free, corn- and soy-free, per the farm. Not certified organic. The farm says it has grown from one farm into a network of small farmers working under the same standards, so not every item is necessarily from the home farm. Product prices are shown only after creating a free account. Contact by phone or the site's contact form; no public email listed.
