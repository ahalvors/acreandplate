Foxhollow Farm

Crestwood, KY
angus

Genetics

Closed herd of about 550 cattle, mostly brown-hided crossbreds about 2/3 Red Angus and 1/3 Jersey with some Corriente and Barzona, all born on the farm's pastures; 100% grass-fed and grass-finished (primarily alfalfa and orchard grass) with rotational grazing; no growth hormones or grain, antibiotics only as a last resort per the farm

About

Foxhollow Farm is a 1,300-acre family farm in Crestwood, Kentucky, stewarded by the same family for more than 65 years and farmed with biodynamic methods since 2005. Its closed herd grazes 720 acres of pasture the farm says is Demeter Certified Biodynamic, and the beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, processed locally at a USDA-inspected facility.

Note: The farm calls its beef AGA Certified 100% grassfed on its homepage and an American Grassfed Association member on its beef page; pastures are Demeter Certified Biodynamic per the farm. Foxhollow Market (8905 Highway 329, Crestwood, KY 40014) is self-serve daily dawn to dusk and staffed Thu-Fri 10-5 and Sat 10-1. Also sells pork, chicken, bison, lamb and seafood (not all raised on the farm, so check the product). Beef shares listed at $775 (1/8) to $5,250 (whole) on Nov 8, 2026. Checked 2026-11-08.

Shipping

Ships frozen with dry ice via UPS (orders placed by Sunday midnight go out Wednesday, arriving by Friday) to eastern and nearby states only: AL, DE, FL, GA, IL, IN, KY, MD, NJ, NY, NC, OH, PA, SC, TN, VA, WV. Local van delivery near the farm; order pickup at the on-farm Foxhollow Market in Crestwood.
