R&W Livestock

Rio Vista, CA

Genetics

Raised on native grasses and high-quality hay for the majority of their lives, with spent brewer's grain added in the final feeding stage (the ranch notes this is not traditional corn/grain feeding); all beef born, raised and finished locally per the ranch

About

Family ranch in Rio Vista (Solano County) founded by Joanna & Aaron Wilson, selling ranch-direct beef shares, curated boxes and individual cuts. Boxes are hand-picked, with a build-your-own option.

Note: Operations address 2772 Olsen Rd, Rio Vista, CA 94571 (visits by appointment only). Phone 707-685-2667, 9am-5pm. Half/whole shares start with a Request Quote form; share buyers choose cuts with the processor. Breed and certifications not stated on the site; cattle are not strictly grass-finished (brewer's grain finish). Email rwlivestockco@gmail.com per site footer.

Products Available

  • half and whole beef shares (non-refundable deposit, balance due at harvest on hanging weight)
  • curated beef boxes
  • beef à la carte (steaks, roasts, brisket, prime rib, organs, bones, suet)

Shipping

Beef is delivered to your door in a temperature-controlled refrigerated vehicle per the ranch; delivery area and fees aren't stated, so ask the ranch. Ranch visits by appointment only.
