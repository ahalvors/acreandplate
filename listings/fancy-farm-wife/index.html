Fancy Farm Wife

Raymond, OH
HighlandAngus

Genetics

Mostly Scottish Highland cattle with some Angus, kept on a grass-only diet; the farm describes its beef as 100% grass-fed, pasture-raised and grass-finished from slow-grown heritage cattle, never from a feedlot

About

Fancy Farm Wife is Russ and Amanda's family farm in Raymond, Ohio, which began with one Highland cow in 2019. It raises mostly Scottish Highland cattle, plus some Angus, on grass only, and sells individual cuts, monthly boxes and freezer beef with free home delivery across Ohio on larger orders.

Note: Farm store: The Fancy Farm Wife Mercantile, 22193 Herd Mcilroy Road, Raymond, OH 43067; pickup Mon-Fri 5:30-7 PM and Sun 1-4:30 PM, call ahead. Bulk beef questions: Russ at (937) 209-5177. Public prices seen 2026-11-11 include Highland ribeye $26.50, NY strip $42.25 and Farmer's Choice boxes from $165/month (10 lb); freezer beef is priced by hanging weight with a deposit (quarter share deposit $300) plus about $1.10/lb hanging weight paid to a state-inspected processor. The farm says its beef is Certified Quality Highland Beef (QHB) verified by the American Highland Cattle Association; that is the farm's own claim and has not been independently checked. Also sells Berkshire-cross pork, free-range eggs, farm apparel and livestock hauling. Checked 2026-11-11.

Products Available

  • ribeye, strip, T-bone, Delmonico, Denver, sirloin and tenderloin steaks
  • ground beef
  • stew meat
  • patties and chopped steak
  • fajita strips
  • shredded pre-cooked beef
  • carnivore diet bundles
  • monthly Farmer's Choice boxes
  • quarter, half and whole freezer beef

Shipping

Local delivery only: free home delivery in Ohio on orders of $200+, delivered personally by the farmers rather than by UPS/USPS. Farm pickup in Raymond, Ohio (call ahead).
