Genetics

About

Jordan Farm is a family-run grassfed beef farm in South Dartmouth, on the South Coast of Massachusetts. Its closed Hereford herd is rotationally grazed on about 70 acres of clover and grass pasture, with winter hay grown in local fields, and the farm practices no-till field rotation and offers seaweed licks. The farm says it uses no growth hormones, antibiotics, or chemical de-wormers, and no pesticides, herbicides, or non-organic fertilizers. It is Certified Grassfed and Animal Welfare Approved by A Greener World, and certified Tier 3 regenerative by Regenified.

Products Available

  • whole, half and quarter cow with pre-set cut lists (offal and bones included free on request)
  • steaks (tenderloin, porterhouse, T-bone, bone-in ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, flank, London broil)
  • ground beef and burger patties
  • everyday and cooking cuts (chuck, round, stew meat and more)
