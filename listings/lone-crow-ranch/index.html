About

First-generation ranch (Cody & Jen Hoseth) in Eltopia raising Wagyu and Angus cattle with own USDA butcher shop in Connell. 1,000 head of cattle with no added hormones, diet formulated by certified animal nutritionist. Locally sourced feed including non-GMO wheat, apples, and potatoes.
