Koopmann Family Beef

Sunol, CA

Genetics

All-natural pasture-raised; cattle spend over 90% of lives on Bay Area hills forage (grass); free of hormones and antibiotics; offers grass-fed and grass-raised grain-finished

About

Fifth-generation Bay Area cattle family (Clayton & Natalie Koopmann) selling ranch-direct all-natural beef grazed in Northern California / SF Bay hills. Managed grazing and conservation. Founded 2020 as retail brand.

Note: Ranch pickup at 9480 Koopmann Road, Sunol, CA 94586 (usually Mon & Fri 11am–3pm, subject to change). Also at Livermore farmers markets. Currently only taking bulk orders for 2025. Contact: koopmannfamilybeef@gmail.com

Products Available

  • seasonings coming soon
