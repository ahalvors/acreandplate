About

Family farm in Lehi, Utah producing locally raised beef since 1908. Grass-fed and grain-finished cattle raised with care, without unnecessary antibiotics or hormones. Custom cuts prepared and vacuum-sealed by a USDA-certified butcher, dry-aged for 14-21 days for superior tenderness and flavor.
