Genetics

Black Angus; grass-fed year-round on pasture and local alfalfa, finished on grass-and-barley with extended 180+ day finishing time; no corn; GMO-free; 100% free from antibiotics, growth-hormones, and ionophores; whole carcass dry-aged 14-28 days; USDA-inspected

About

Family ranch in Fort Jones, California raising Black Angus cattle (also known as Five Marys Farms). Cattle live on pastures or hill country year-round eating local pasture grasses and alfalfa, finished on a balanced ration of alfalfa, grain hay, and steam-flaked barley for at least 180 days. Whole carcass dry-aged 14-28 days, GMO-free and corn-free. Ships nationwide frozen.

Note: Family ranch run by Brian & Mary Heffernan and their four daughters (all named Mary); also marketed as Five Marys Farms; bought historic Sharps Gulch Ranch in 2013; raises the majority of their cattle (some weaned calves sourced from family and friends). Also raises Berkshire pigs and Navajo Churro lambs. Contact: info@fivemarys.com. Address: 11903 Main Street, Fort Jones, CA 96032.
