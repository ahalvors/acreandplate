Farmer's Provisions

Middletown, IN

Genetics

Angus-based herd raised by the Howell family in Middletown, Indiana; per the farm, cattle are 100% grass-fed and grass-finished on grasses, legumes and brassicas, never fed grain or grain by-products, moved to fresh pasture daily, with no added hormones

About

Farmer's Provisions is the Howell family's farm a short drive from Indianapolis, where the family has raised livestock and crops for more than 50 years across two generations. Its Angus-based cattle are 100% grass-fed and grass-finished with daily rotational grazing on pasture and cover crops, and the dry-aged beef is processed at a local family-owned USDA-inspected butcher and shipped frozen from the farm.
