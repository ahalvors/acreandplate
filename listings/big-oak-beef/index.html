Big Oak Beef (Nix Family Farm)

Gainesville, GA
HerefordAngus

Genetics

Grass-fed and grass-finished Black Baldy cattle (Hereford cows crossed with an Aberdeen Angus bull), raised on mother's milk then pasture forage (fescue, Bermuda, orchard and rye grasses) with hay in winter; no hormones, steroids or antibiotics per the farm

About

The Nix family has raised cattle in north Georgia since 1969, when Larry Nix bought the first 40 acres after military service. A small Hereford herd crossed with an Angus bull produces grass-fed, grass-finished Black Baldy beef sold by the whole, half, quarter or box.

Note: Official Georgia Grown producer per the site. Farm address 3776 Anglin Drive, Gainesville, GA 30507. Prices not listed on the site; no certifications claimed. Checked 2026-11-07.

Products Available
