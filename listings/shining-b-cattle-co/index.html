Shining B Cattle Co.

Genetics

About

Four generations of family ranching in Pleasanton, CA. Buy directly from the family. Cattle are grass-grazed and grain-finished with a custom program developed with a beef nutritionist. Hormone free, ethically raised.

Note: Quarter/half/whole beef shares with deposits $500/$1,000/$1,500 (currently sold out). Half beef $3,270 including processing and cut & wrap. Harvested summer and fall. Average yields: whole 400-425 lb, half 200-225 lb, quarter 100-115 lb. Whole/half include custom cuts, quarter standard cuts. Individual cuts available in online store. Instagram: @shiningbcattleco_
