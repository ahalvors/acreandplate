Etta Hills Farm

Etta, MS

Genetics

Breed not stated. The farm says the beef is 100% grass-finished, on pasture from birth through the last day, with rotational grazing and no hormones, antibiotics, or grain finishing. The farm says every protein is sent to an independent lab for phytonutrient-density testing

About

Etta Hills Farm is in Etta, Mississippi, about 20 minutes south of Oxford. Established in 2021 on about 800 acres of degraded north Mississippi farmland the farm says it has taken back and regenerated. Co-founder Dr. Allen Williams is a sixth-generation farmer and Ph.D. animal scientist who co-founded Understanding Ag, Soil Health Academy, and Regenified. Chris Williams is co-owner and director of operations. The beef is 100% grass-finished and kept on pasture from birth through the last day. The farm also raises pastured pork, poultry, eggs, and grass-finished lamb.
