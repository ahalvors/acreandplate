Yankee Farmer's Market (Pitcher Mountain Farm)

Warner, NH
Heritagescottish-highland

Genetics

Scottish Highland cattle raised 100% grass-fed and grass-finished on open pasture at Pitcher Mountain Farm, with no hormones or antibiotics per the farm; some beef comes from partner local farms

About

Family-run New Hampshire meat farm started in 1998 by Keira and Brian Farmer, who sold their car to buy their first five buffalo. They now raise bison, Scottish Highland cattle and pastured pigs across three farm locations, with the cattle on pasture at Pitcher Mountain Farm, and run a year-round farm store in Warner. Cattle graze rotated pastures, and the meat is USDA inspected.

Note: Phone 603-456-2833, info@yankeefarmersmarket.com. The farm says it also partners with trusted local farms for more selection, so not every beef cut is necessarily from its own Highland herd. Grass-finished and no-hormones/antibiotics claims are the farm's own. Farm store open Monday to Thursday 10 to 5 and Saturday 9 to 4.

Products Available

  • steaks (ribeye, bone-in ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, tenderloin)
  • steak tips, kabobs, shaved steak and stew meat
  • ground beef and burgers
  • roasts, brisket, short ribs, back ribs and osso buco
  • 1/4, 1/2 and whole beef packages, plus a Beef Freezer Teaser and Beef Bundle
  • pasture-raised bison (buffalo), the farm's original herd

Shipping

Ships frozen by UPS anywhere in the continental U.S. (orders go out Monday to Wednesday and arrive in 2 days or less); nationwide shipping on most bulk packages. Also pickup at the farm store, 360 Route 103 E, Warner
