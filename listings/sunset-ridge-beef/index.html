About

Family ranch in New York's Butternut Valley raising USDA Certified, New York Grown & Certified all-natural Angus beef. Pasture-raised with rotational grazing, handled with low stress for better quality meat. Processed and vacuum-sealed, shipped frozen nationwide.

Note: Zrowka Family Ranch in New York's Butternut Valley. USDA Certified. Rotational grazing for sustainable ranching. Flash-frozen cryovacked beef products vacuum-sealed. 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, and whole beef shares available.
