Nick's Organic Farm

Adamstown, MD
angus

Genetics

Black Angus. The farm says the cattle are 100% grass-fed their entire lives and never fed grain, kept on pasture all day, every day and never housed indoors, then fed the farm's hay and baleage after the growing season, plus well water and mineral salts mixed with sea kelp, and are not fed hormones, GMOs, antibiotics, or animal products. The farm says the pastures and the hay are certified organic

About

Nick's Organic Farm is a family farm near Buckeystown, in Maryland's Frederick Valley, a few miles south of Frederick. Nick Maravell started the farm on a commercial basis in 1979 and bought the 175-acre farm in 1997. The farm says the land is protected from development by a Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation easement. The farm says its Black Angus cattle are 100% grass-fed and rotated across the pastures, and that the beef is dry-aged to the farm's specifications at a USDA-inspected family-run processor, then sold frozen by the share.

Products Available

  • whole, half, quarter, and eighth beef shares by hanging weight (frozen; dry-aged; every share cut from split halves so customers get the same cuts)
  • additional lean ground beef (80-85%) and ground-beef patties from a bulk share
  • hanger steak, organs (liver, heart, tongue, tail, cheeks, kidneys, sweetbreads), marrow and soup bones, suet, and trim fat
  • all-beef sausage (country, chorizo, Italian, kielbasa, sage, plain, and breakfast links) and beef jerky
  • pastured chicken, eggs, and Thanksgiving turkeys
