Polyface Farm

Swoope, VA

Genetics

100% grass-fed beef; cattle moved daily on fresh pasture with portable electric fencing (the farm's "salad bar beef"); no vaccines or antibiotics per the farm; not USDA certified organic (the farm describes itself as "Beyond Organic"); breed not named on the site

About

Salatin family farm in Virginia's Shenandoah Valley near Staunton, bought by William and Lucille Salatin in 1961 and now worked by three generations. Regenerative, grass-based farming with daily cow moves, compost and no synthetic fertilizers, pesticides or GMOs; visitors welcome any time.

Note: Address 43 Pure Meadows Ln, Swoope, VA 24479; phone 540-885-3590 and Wendy@polyfacefarm.com per the site footer. Shipping terms per the site's How to Buy page (Nov 2, 2026). The farm says some products come from nearby partner farms and are labeled as such. Beef prices are not public (account required), so none are listed. No breed or third-party certification claimed.

Products Available

  • 100% grass-fed beef
  • pastured pork
  • pastured chicken and turkey
  • eggs, dairy and pantry items
  • Subscribe & Save (5% off)

Shipping

Ships frozen via UPS to the contiguous US every week: UPS 1-2 day Ground ($25 delivery fee, free over $175, $50 minimum) or UPS 2-Day Air ($35 fee, $50 minimum); also on-farm store and pickup locations/neighborhood delivery clubs across the region (pickup has no minimum and the lowest prices).
