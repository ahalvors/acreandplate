The Farm at Spring Creek

Greeneville, TN

Genetics

Pasture-raised and pasture-finished (grass-fed and finished); cattle are never fed grain, with farm-cut supplemental hay in winter and minerals year-round; no hormones or antibiotics per the farm; dry-aged

About

A four-generation family farm in the hills of East Tennessee, run by the Turner and Weems families, raising grass-fed and grass-finished, dry-aged beef processed with regional partners and sold direct from the farm.

Note: Breed not named on the site (breeds empty). Also sells tallow soap, raw honey, eggs, seasonal vegetables, and log cabin farm stays. Prices from the farm's beef shop page when checked 2026-11-06; confirm current pricing with the farm.

Products Available

  • half beef share ($200 deposit; $6.75/lb hanging weight)
  • whole beef share ($400 deposit; $6.75/lb hanging weight)
  • ground beef (dry-aged 14 days)
  • steaks (filet, cube steak)
  • roasts and brisket
  • organ meats, oxtail, bones

Shipping

Ships beef to your door almost anywhere in the U.S. per the farm, or pick up locally in Greeneville, TN. Bulk share price includes local delivery. Shipping rates not stated.
