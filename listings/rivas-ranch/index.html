Rivas Ranch

Tobaccoville, NC

Genetics

Breed not stated. The ranch says its beef is grass-fed and grass-finished on its North Carolina ranch, with no grain lot at the end. Every share comes from one animal and is never co-mingled, processed one animal at a time at a local, family-owned USDA-inspected plant. Halal processing is available on request

About

Rivas Ranch is the Rivas family's ranch in Tobaccoville, North Carolina, run by April Rivas, a food safety educator and inspector who cooked professionally before raising cattle. They raise grass-fed, grass-finished beef, plus lamb, goat, pork, and chicken on pasture, and sell beef by the share, in set-price boxes, and as one shipped quarter.

Note: Checked 2026-11-21 on rivasranch.com, https://rivasranch.com/pages/shop-beef, and https://rivasranch.com/pages/contact. Beef by the pound of hanging weight: quarter $9.25/lb (about 150-175 lb hanging, about $1,390-$1,620, $500 deposit), half $8.75/lb (about 300-325 lb hanging, about $2,630-$2,840, $950 deposit), whole $8.25/lb (about 600-650 lb hanging, about $4,950-$5,360, $1,200 deposit). Deposits are not refundable and come off the final invoice. The balance is invoiced from the processor's hanging weight, plus a 2% NC food tax, and is due within 3 days. Take-home weight is about 55-65% of hanging weight (a quarter is about 80-115 lb packaged). Set-Price Box: quarter $1,399 (at least 75 lb) and half $2,699 (at least 150 lb); pay in full, or half now and half at processing. Shipped Quarter: 70 lb for $1,500 with shipping included to the lower 48 and DC; shipping is not refundable. Ground beef boxes: 10 lb $90 and 40 lb $340. Next beef processing date shown: Dec 15, 2026, with 1 quarter share left on that date. Beef is ready about 3 weeks after processing. Address: 6981 Doral Dr, Tobaccoville, NC 27050. Text the ranch: 336-747-3070. Email: hello@rivasranch.com. The homepage lists freezer space as about 5 cu ft for a quarter, 10 for a half, and 20 for a whole; the shop-beef page says about 3-4 cu ft for a by-the-pound quarter, about 3 cu ft for a Set-Price quarter, about 8-10 for a half, and a full-size chest freezer or two for a whole. The homepage says Greensboro Curb Market; the shop-beef page says Greensboro Farmers Curb Market. Pork also ships frozen, with shipping billed when it ships. Skincare and pet treats ship within 3 business days. Halal is available for beef, lamb, goat, and chicken; beef bacon and beef sausage are not available halal. Breed is not stated. No certifications are claimed. The processor is not named.

Products Available

  • quarter cow, half cow, and whole cow by the pound of hanging weight
  • Set-Price Box (quarter or half; Family, Grill, or Rancher + Chef)
  • Shipped Quarter box
  • ground beef boxes
  • pasture-raised lamb, goat, pork, and chicken; tallow skincare made on the ranch; Two Barking Dogs pet snacks

Shipping

Only the Shipped Quarter ships: 70 lb frozen to the lower 48 and DC, shipping included. By-the-pound shares are pickup or delivery, not shipped. Free door delivery anywhere in North Carolina on a monthly run (third week of the month). Free pickup at the farm in Tobaccoville by appointment (5-7 PM daily), at the Greensboro Farmers Curb Market, or at a Ranch Route stop. The Ranch Route also stops in parts of Virginia, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New York, South Carolina, and Georgia.
