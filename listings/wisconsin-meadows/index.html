Genetics

About

Farmer-owned cooperative of 230+ Wisconsin family farms raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef with regenerative practices. Low-stress handling and rotational grazing protect soil and water resources. All business decisions made by member farmers, profits returned to farmers. Cryovac-sealed, frozen, and delivered to your door.
