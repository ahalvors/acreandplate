About

White Oak Pastures is a six-generation family farm in Bluffton, Georgia, established in 1866. Focus on regenerative land management, humane animal husbandry, and rural revitalization. All cattle are grass-fed and grass-finished, certified by the American Grassfed Association. Processed in on-farm USDA-inspected abattoir with zero-waste practices. Hand-butchered beef available direct to consumers nationwide.

Note: Six-generation family farm established 1866. On-farm USDA-inspected abattoir with zero-waste processing. On-farm store open 7 days a week (Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sat 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sun 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM). Also sells pastured chicken, heritage pork, grassfed lamb, grassfed goat, and more. Free shipping on ground orders over $249; $15 perishable shipping on orders over $149. Farmer Will Harris wrote 'A Bold Return To Giving A Damn'. Email: info@whiteoakpastures.com.
