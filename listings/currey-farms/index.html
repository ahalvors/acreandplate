Currey Farms

Harrington, DE

Genetics

Grass-fed, grain-finished beef from cattle the family breeds themselves. Cattle spend most of their time grazing pasture, eat hay and forages grown on the farm, and are finished with non-GMO grain the farm grinds itself. No antibiotics, growth hormones, GMOs or steroids (per the farm)

About

Fourth-generation family farm in Harrington, Delaware, founded in 1942 and now run by Will and Molly Currey, who breed their own cattle and sell grass-fed, grain-finished beef directly from the farm by the cut, in packages and in quarter, half and whole shares.

Note: Phone 302-245-3464, curreyfarms@gmail.com. Beef is grain-finished on farm-ground non-GMO grain, not grass-finished. No-antibiotics, no-hormones and non-GMO claims are the farm's own; no organic or third-party certification is claimed. Breed isn't named on the site. Prices shown are from the farm's beef page on Oct 29, 2026 and may change.

Products Available

  • individual cuts priced per lb: steaks (ribeye, NY strip, filet, porterhouse, T-bone, sirloin, flat iron, skirt, flank), roasts, brisket, short ribs, ground and stew meat
  • organs and soup bones (heart, liver, tongue, oxtail)
  • 10 lb ground beef package ($54) and $400 Freezer Filler package
  • quarter, half and whole beef at $5.25 per lb dress weight
  • hay and Priefert ranch equipment
