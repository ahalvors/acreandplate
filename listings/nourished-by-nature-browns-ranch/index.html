Nourished By Nature (Brown's Ranch)

Bismarck, ND

Genetics

All beef born and raised on the family's own ranch east of Bismarck (no outsourcing from other ranches); 100% grass-fed and grass-finished on a diverse diet of grass and forbs, no grain ever; on pasture year-round; Tier 5 Regenified certified per the farm

About

Regenerative family ranch just east of Bismarck, North Dakota, run by Paul and Jazmin Brown on the ranch Gabe and Shelly Brown bought in 1991. The beef is born and raised on the ranch, 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, and raised with no vaccines, dewormers, antibiotics, hormones or GMOs. Pastures get about 360 days of recovery between grazings, and the ranch has used no-till farming since 1993 and no synthetic fertilizer since 2009. Beef is wet-aged 30 days.

Note: Cattle breed not named on the ranch's website. Prices show only after creating an account. The Regenified and drug-free claims are the ranch's own. Email listed is the farm pickup contact (Jazmin Brown); the site also has a contact form.

Products Available

  • steaks (ribeye, NY strip, tenderloin filet, hanger, flat-iron, flank, coulotte, teres major, sirloin)
  • roasts (prime rib, chuck, rump, arm, sirloin tip, tri tip) and brisket
  • ground beef (several lean ratios) and patties
  • short ribs, oxtail, shanks, marrow and soup bones
  • organ meats, tallow, suet and bone broth
  • beef snack sticks, summer sausage, beef bacon and hot dogs
  • beef pet food
  • bulk beef bundles (1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, whole) and ranch boxes
  • pastured pork, chicken, turkey, lamb and eggs
