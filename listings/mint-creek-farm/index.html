Mint Creek Farm

Stelle, IL
angus

Genetics

Small herd (under 60 head) of Black and Red Angus cattle raised from birth on the farm's 220 acres of organic perennial pasture; 100% grass-fed and grass-finished with an organically approved mineral mix, no grain, no growth hormones and no antibiotics per the farm; barn shelter and certified-organic hay in winter

About

Mint Creek Farm is an organic, pasture-based livestock farm near Stelle, Illinois, founded by the Carr family in 1992. Its small herd of Black and Red Angus cattle is raised from birth on 220 acres of perennial pasture and is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, with weekly home delivery and farmers market pickup across the Chicago area.

Note: Mailing address: 1693 East 3800N Road, Cabery, IL 60919. Order by Tuesday noon for Wednesday home delivery or that weekend's market pickup; bulk orders such as quarter cows and CSA shares can take 3-6 weeks. Public prices seen 2026-11-12 include ground beef $17/lb, NY strip $28, ribeye from $30 and a 6-steak specialty bundle $166. The farm describes its pastures as organic and its winter hay as certified organic; no organic certification is shown on the beef itself, so none is claimed here. Also sells pork, lamb, goat, pastured turkeys and eggs, and hosts farm dinners and tours. Checked 2026-11-12.

Products Available

  • ribeye, NY strip, filet mignon, T-bone, sirloin, hanger, skirt, flank and flat iron steaks
  • chuck, rump and eye of round roasts, tri-tip and brisket
  • ground beef, ground beef with liver and burger patties
  • short ribs, oxtail, osso buco shank, kabob and stew meat
  • summer sausage and snack sticks
  • steak bundles and bulk ground beef packs
  • monthly meat CSA shares

Shipping

Farm-van home delivery every Wednesday in Chicago, Oak Park and Evanston ($9 per delivery, no minimum). UPS shipping outside that area starts at $25 per delivery (UPS/FedEx costs quoted on request). Pickup at Chicago-area farmers markets and curbside pickup points.
