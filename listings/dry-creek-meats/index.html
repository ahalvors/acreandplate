Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished; zero corn, soy, or grain by-products; regenerative ranching; cattle graze freely on a 23,000-acre ranch in New Mexico then finish at the western Oklahoma ranch; no antibiotics, no growth hormones; grazing acres never treated with chemical fertilizers or herbicides (site claims)

About

Three-generation Riley family regenerative ranch raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef across New Mexico and western Oklahoma (Leedey). Ranch-direct retail cuts, beef shares, boxes, and subscriptions with nationwide shipping, OKC-area delivery, and ranch pickup.
