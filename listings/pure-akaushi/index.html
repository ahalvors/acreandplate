Pure Akaushi

Boise, ID
Akaushi

Genetics

The ranch describes its cattle as 100% Pure Akaushi genetics with no crossbreeding. 100% Fullblood Akaushi Wagyu, pasture-raised and grass-fed on regenerative Idaho pastures. The ranch says cattle are raised without hormones, antibiotics, or GMOs, with humane treatment certification and USDA certification.

About

Pure Akaushi is a family-run ranch in Boise, Idaho, raising 100% Fullblood Akaushi Wagyu cattle on regenerative pastures. The ranch emphasizes purity (no crossbreeding), integrity (no hormones or antibiotics), sustainability (regenerative practices), and limited-quantity harvests with hands-on stewardship.

Note: Checked 2026-11-27 from pureakaushi.com (home, about, shop pages). The ranch says it raises cattle grass-fed and pasture-raised from start to finish with a limited herd. USDA certified, non-GMO, and humane treatment certified. Specific cuts and pricing are shown on the shop page; product details were not fully accessible during verification. The ranch mentions high levels of heart-healthy oleic acid and omega-3s. Located at 404 South 8th Street, Boise, Idaho 83702.
