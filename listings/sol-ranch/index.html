Sol Ranch

Wagon Mound, NM

Genetics

Grassfed and grass-finished; cattle spend their entire lives on pasture in northeastern New Mexico; dry-aged; raised, finished, and processed in New Mexico per the ranch

About

Sol Ranch LLC in Wagon Mound raises grassfed and finished dry-aged beef where the plains meet the mountains in northeastern New Mexico, using adaptive ranching practices aimed at soil health and watershed resiliency.

Note: No public phone on the site (emails beefsales@solranchnm.com and solranchnm@gmail.com; hours Sat 8am–5pm). Breed not named (breeds empty). Also sells lamb. Short ribs special was sold out on the purchase page when checked 2026-11-05. Site lists retail partners in NM/CO; this directory listing is for ranch-direct DTC.

Products Available

  • half beef share (deposit)
  • bulk beef from 1/16th to whole
  • ground beef
  • mixed steak bundles
  • slow-cookin' beef bundles
  • organ meats, tongue, oxtail, bones

Shipping

Local delivery in New Mexico to Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Cedar Crest, Taos, Las Vegas, Watrous, Springer, and Raton, plus Trinidad, CO (about once a month); free Wagon Mound delivery on a pre-scheduled basis. Delivery rates on the ranch purchase page. No nationwide shipping stated.
