Genetics

About

Fifth-generation family farm (Knute & Amanda Severson) in Clarion, Iowa raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef since 2017. American Grassfed Association third-party verified. Dry-aged 14 days. Regenerative farming with intensive rotational grazing builds soil health, captures carbon, and restores wildlife habitat.
