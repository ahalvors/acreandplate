About

Family-owned regenerative farm run by Dustin and Erin Schnabel raising single-origin 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef in Holmes County, Ohio. Cattle managed with holistic rotational grazing practices on leased pastureland. Ships next-day across Ohio with farm store pickup available in Canton.

Note: Cattle managed on leased land in Holmes County, Ohio; farm store and pickup at Canton location. Also sells pasture-raised chicken, woodland pork, A2 milk, and dairy products. 100% of profits reinvested into regenerative farming practices. Email: zoefarmsohio@gmail.com. Address: 5676 Faircrest ST SW, Canton, OH 44706.
