Brookshire Farm

Abbeville, LA

Genetics

Calves born on the farm and raised at their mothers' sides; 100% grass-fed and pasture-finished on native and selected cultivated forages, never supplemented with grain; cows selected for calves that grass-finish well and have easy-going dispositions

About

Family farm in Abbeville (Vermilion Parish), Louisiana, selling grass-fed, pasture-finished beef direct through its own online store. Cattle spend their whole lives on forage on rotationally managed pastures, are never supplemented with grain, and are raised with no growth stimulants, antibiotics or other synthetic additives. Beef is dry aged 14 to 21 days in a temperature-controlled cooler.

Note: Breed not named on the farm's website. 2026 beef shares are sold out (newsletter list for the next presale), and many individual cuts showed sold out on Oct 21, 2026. Pickup-location list loads by ZIP code on the site, so specific towns aren't listed here. Contact form on the site; no public email.

Products Available

  • steaks (ribeye, NY strip, filet mignon, sirloin, hanger, skirt, flank, bavette)
  • roasts (rib, ribeye, tenderloin, chuck, rump)
  • ground beef and stew meat
  • short ribs, oxtail, osso buco and marrow bones
  • organ meats, including assorted organs for pet food
  • bulk stock bones
  • beef shares (ground-beef packs, 1/4, 1/2, whole by deposit)
  • pastured pork and chicken
