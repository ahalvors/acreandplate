Sugar Hill Farmstead

Pepeekeo, HI

Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef raised on the family's own pasture on the Hamakua Coast of Hawaii Island; rotational grazing, no added growth hormones, antibiotics or vaccines per the farm

About

Family farm on the Hamakua Coast of Hawaii Island run by Bodhi and Brittany Anderson; Bodhi is a sixth-generation kama'aina carrying on his great-grandfather's farming legacy there. They rebuilt former sweet potato and ginger land with rotational grazing for cattle, sheep, pigs and chickens, and were named Hamakua District Farmer/Rancher of the Year by the USDA in 2023. The beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished and harvested by the family, and the farm runs fully off-grid on solar and wind with rainwater catchment.

Note: Cattle breed not named on the farm's website. Ships only within Hawaii. Many individual cuts showed sold out tonight; beef shares and boxes were available. The hormone-, antibiotic- and vaccine-free claims are the farm's own.

Products Available

  • beef shares (1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and whole, plus low-histamine half and whole shares)
  • beef boxes and bundles (Family Farm Beef Box, Favorites Box, Slow Cooker Sampler, Beef Roast Sampler, ground beef bundles)
  • ground beef and Primal Ground (dry-aged ground beef blended with liver and heart)
  • roasts, brisket, stew beef, oxtail and beef tongue
  • marrow bones, bone broth bundles and beef tallow
  • grass-fed raw beef pet food
  • pastured pork, chicken, lamb, goat, rabbit and eggs
  • wild-caught Alaskan seafood and a monthly CSA subscription

Shipping

Ships frozen by FedEx within Hawaii only (Hawaii Island, Oahu, Maui, Kauai and Molokai); shipping starts at a 20 lb order and beef shares can take up to 2 weeks to ship. Farm pickup and local delivery on Hawaii Island. No mainland shipping
