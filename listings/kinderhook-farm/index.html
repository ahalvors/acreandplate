Kinderhook Farm

Valatie, NY

Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished cattle raised exclusively on diverse pasture forages and hay, with rotational grazing on preserved Hudson Valley farmland that was converted from a conventional dairy to pasture-based beef in 2004 per the farm

About

Kinderhook Farm is a Hudson Valley livestock farm in Columbia County, New York, where a former conventional dairy was restored to pasture in 2004. Its beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, sold by the cut at an on-farm store open seven days a week and in quarter, half and whole shares for pickup.

Note: Public prices seen 2026-11-14: Quarter Cow $955 (~75 lb, about 8 weeks' prep); half beef $7.25/lb and whole beef $7/lb hanging weight (average half about $2,356) with a $200/$400 deposit; MeatShare $650/year or $350/half year (2026 sign-ups currently show out of stock). Bulk beef is processed at a USDA-inspected facility the farm describes as Animal Welfare Approved, and the farm calls its MeatShare meats Animal Welfare Approved; that is the farm's own claim. Cattle breed is not stated on the farm's site. Also sells grass-fed lamb, pastured pork, chicken and eggs, and runs an on-farm Farm Stay. Call or text the farm store number for orders. Checked 2026-11-14.

Products Available

  • Quarter Cow package (~75 lb: ribeye, T-bone, NY strip, sirloin, flat iron, Denver, coulotte, flap, flank/skirt, tri-tip and chuck eye steaks, brisket or chuck roast, short ribs, osso buco, rolled roast, kielbasa, hot dogs, burger patties, stew meat, ground beef and soup bones)
  • half and whole beef, custom cut to your cutsheet
  • grass-fed beef soup bones, suet and beef bone broth
  • year-round whole-farm MeatShare CSA (beef, pork, lamb, chicken and eggs)
  • farm store cuts sold in person, with holiday roasts by reservation

Shipping

Pickup only at the farm store, 1958 County Route 21, Valatie, NY (open 7 days, 9am-5pm). The farm says bulk boxes cannot be delivered.
