Genetics

About

Sonoma coast ranch in Bodega raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef on certified organic pasture, including Scottish Highland and other British breeds suited to steep coastal hills. Family operation selling ranch-direct retail cuts nationwide and local beef shares for Sonoma County pickup.

Note: Order via online store, email info@salmoncreekranch.com, or phone/text. Address published as PO Box 266, 1400 Bay Hill Rd, Bodega, CA 94922. Dry-aged 14-21 days, USDA-inspected vacuum-packed retail cuts. Confirm current inventory and pricing on their beef price list / store before ordering.
