Flying B Beef

Catheys Valley, CA

Genetics

Black Angus; bred, born, raised, grazed, and finished in California; 100% grass-fed and pasture-raised in central California foothills; grain-finished year-round (grain mix, alfalfa, oat hay for ~130-145 days) or grass-finished whole shares (once yearly, May-June); rotational grazing

About

Family-owned beef operation (Austin and Lauren Miller) in Catheys Valley, Mariposa County, California. Black Angus cattle raised in the central California foothills with rotational grazing. Grain-finished and grass-finished options available.

Note: USDA-inspected processing in Atwater, CA; cut and wrapped at Western Meats, Modesto. Quarter share (Baron Box) is pre-packaged; half and whole shares include custom cut sheet.

Products Available

  • beef boxes (Urbanite, Ranchette, Doublewide, Burger Box, Homesteader, Baron Box)
  • bulk shares (quarter/half/whole)

Shipping

Local pickup at Mae It Be Home, downtown Mariposa, CA; free delivery on orders $1,000+ within ~1 hour of Catheys Valley (approx. Clovis to Modesto area)

Website: https://flyingbbeef.com/
