About

Fifth-generation Kansas family ranch raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef with regenerative practices. Cattle never given hormones, antibiotics, or grain. Ships nationwide from Johnson City with farm-to-table transparency.

Note: Fifth-generation family ranch (Matt and Michelle Canny) in Stanton County, rural Kansas. Regenerative ranching with pasture rotation. Also operates farm-to-table restaurant at 112 South Main Street. Contact: hello@whollycowmarket.com. Address: 303 South Main St. Johnson, KS 67855.
