K5 Wagyu

Doyle, TN
Wagyu

Genetics

Registered Fullblood Black Wagyu (the ranch says 100% Wagyu genetics, registered and traceable to Japanese bloodlines) and F1 Wagyu (the ranch describes F1 as a first-generation cross of a purebred Wagyu sire and a high-quality Angus or other British breed dam). Cattle graze Tennessee pasture. Finishing method is not stated.

About

K5 Wagyu is the beef brand of Kolster Cattle Company, a family-owned ranch in Middle Tennessee raising Fullblood Black Wagyu and F1 Wagyu and selling beef direct by the cut, by the pound, or by the package. The ranch says it was founded by two United States Marine Corps veterans and remains entirely family-owned and operated.

Products Available

  • steaks (filet mignon, ribeye, Delmonico, NY strip, picanha, Denver)
  • ribs and roasts (center cut ribs, chuck ribs, picanha roast)
  • ground Wagyu, fajita strips, shaved steak, kabobs, primal blend, burger patties
  • quarter, half, and whole-animal packages

Shipping

Local pickup at the farm in Middle Tennessee, or delivery within Tennessee. The ranch says it does not ship outside Tennessee at this time. Orders are vacuum-sealed, frozen, and labeled by cut and weight. No online cart; order by call, text, or inquiry.
