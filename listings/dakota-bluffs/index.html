Dakota Bluffs

Platte, SD

Genetics

Breed not stated. The ranch says its cattle are 100% grass-finished and never grain-fed: on pasture all summer, fed hay over winter, then back on spring grass where they are finished. The ranch describes regenerative grazing, low-stress handling, no feedlots, no unnecessary antibiotics, and no synthetic hormones

About

Dakota Bluffs is Doug and Cindy Lucas's family farm near Platte, South Dakota, on prairie grassland at the foot of the Bijou Hills a few miles from the Missouri River. They moved to the farm in 2016 and say it is the original homestead, in the family for five generations. Their beef is 100% grass-finished and sold direct, shipped from the farm. Through a Buy One, Give One program, each beef sold helps a school overseas buy a cow from a local farmer, an idea from Cindy's years teaching in West Africa.

Note: Checked 2026-11-20 on dakotabluffs.com (home, about, contact, product pages). The bulk share product pages say 'Deposits opening soon!', so share deposits may not be open yet. Share pricing is per lb of hanging weight, calculated at delivery: eighth about 100-200 lb hanging and quarter about 200-300 lb at $9.25/lb; half about 300-400 lb and whole about 700-800 lb at $9/lb; the final balance is invoiced before shipping. Packaged meat is typically about 60% of hanging weight. Offers a full refund if you don't like the beef ('Farmer's Handshake'). Processor is described only as a family-owned USDA processor, not named. No phone or email is shown on the ranch's own site (third-party directories list some, not used here). Breed and certifications not stated.

  • Pure Pasture ground beef bundles in 1 lb packs: 10 lb $110 (+$19.99 shipping), 20 lb $215, 45 lb $475, 90 lb $950
  • Taste of the Prairie 20 lb sampler box $380 (first-order Welcome Pack $317)
  • The Mini sampler $152 (3 lb ground, a 3 lb roast, 2 steaks)

Shipping

Ships frozen anywhere in the lower 48 (not Alaska or Hawaii). Shares and most bundles ship free; the 10 lb ground bundle adds $19.99. Whole-beef buyers who pick up at the ranch save $1,000. Shares are harvested in the fall (usually September to November) and fulfilled in the fall
