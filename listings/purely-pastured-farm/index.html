Purely Pastured Farm

Genetics

Breed not stated. The farm says the beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, rotationally grazed, and never given antibiotics, hormones, mRNA, or other vaccinations

About

Purely Pastured Farm is a family farm in Foley, Alabama (Baldwin County), run by Robert (Rob) and Maegen Benford and their three boys. The farm says its beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, rotationally grazed, and never given antibiotics, hormones, mRNA, or other vaccinations. The farm also raises pastured pork, chicken, and eggs.

Note: Public prices seen 2026-11-23: cuts sold by the pound, including filet steak (2") $46.50/lb, chuck eye steak $19/lb, picanha $18/lb, chuck roast $13.25/lb, and brisket $12.75/lb. Steak & Burger Box small (6-8 lb) $125 (regular $150), large (12-14 lb) $229 (regular $250). Quarter, half, and whole shares are reserved by deposit for January processing. A 1/4 share is a $375 deposit at $7.75/lb hanging weight (the farm estimates about 192 lb hanging, about 130-145 lb take-home, about $1,488 for the beef plus about $225 processing paid to the processor). Half and whole deposit amounts are not stated. A 3.5% fee applies to credit cards. Address: 14190 Joe Norris Lane, Foley, AL 36535, Baldwin County. Phone 251-233-9407. Email purelypasturedfarm@gmail.com. Breed is not stated. No certifications are stated. No shipping: farm pickup by appointment in Baldwin County and at select local pickup locations (those locations are not named); home delivery within a 22-mile radius is for Subscribe & Save customers only ($200 minimum, first Monday of the month). Shares are farm pickup. The farm says it serves South Alabama and Northwest Florida (Mobile, Pensacola, Fairhope, Daphne, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach area). Checked 2026-11-23.
