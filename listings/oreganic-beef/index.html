Genetics

About

Family-owned Central Oregon ranch (Rocker 3 Ranch in Madras) selling ranch-direct USDA Certified Organic, 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef under the Oreganic Beef Co. brand. GAP4 certified, fully traceable, dry-aged prime cuts 30+ days.

Note: Brand Oreganic Beef Co. markets beef from Rocker 3 Ranch (Madras, OR; family ranch est. ~1991; DTC brand launched ~2019). Processed at Mark's Meat Co., Canby, OR (USDA organic certified butcher). Emails published: Info@OreganicBeef.com / Devin@OreganicBeef.com. Ranch visits by appointment.
