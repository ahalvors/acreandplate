About

Women-owned & operated family ranch established 2022 in the Sierra Foothills of Mariposa, California. Run by Lacey & Alyssa Sharp. 100% grass-fed beef with no chemical dewormers and no vaccines—ever. Regenerative grazing methods inspired by the Savory Institute, using frequent pasture moves.

Note: Processed at USDA processor Sanders Meats, Turlock, CA. Paid in full at order; 60-90 days typical. Personal delivery same day from processor. Delivery radius not stated. Instagram: @sharpfarmsandcattleco
