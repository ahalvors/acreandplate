Crossroads Beef

Carroll, IA
angus

Genetics

Red Angus herd raised on pasture its entire life; 100% grass-fed and grass-finished with adaptive rotational grazing onto new pasture daily, a planted mix of perennial grasses and legumes, and minerals plus baled grasses in winter. Never in a feedlot, no growth hormones, no GMO feed and no mRNA vaccines per the farm

About

Crossroads Beef is a family farm in Carroll, Iowa, where a 130-year-old row-crop operation switched to raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished Red Angus beef. The herd is moved to fresh pasture daily, never sees a feedlot, and the dry-aged beef ships direct from the farm to 28 states.

Note: Contact is through the farm's website contact form only; no public phone or email is listed. The farm says it follows organic practices but is not USDA-certified organic, so no organic claim is made here. Beef is processed at a USDA-inspected facility; cuts are mostly wrapped in plastic and butcher paper (some vacuum-sealed). Public prices seen 2026-11-13 include ribeye from $24.87, NY strip from $20.17, ground beef from $12 and the Family Essentials 10 lb collection from $133.31. Beef share pricing is set per order. Checked 2026-11-13.

Products Available

  • ribeye, NY strip, tenderloin filet, top sirloin, sirloin tip, chuck eye and hanger steaks
  • chuck blade roasts and short ribs
  • single-source ground beef, burger patties, skinless beef brats and all-beef hot dogs
  • fajita/stir-fry strips, stew meat and NY strip sandwich slices
  • liver and other organ meats, marrow bones, cross-cut shank and suet/tallow
  • Family Essentials 10 lb and 20 lb collections and Daily Grind 16 lb and 30 lb ground beef boxes
  • custom quarter, half and whole beef shares (pickup at the processor)

Shipping

Ships frozen with dry ice on Mondays or Tuesdays (1-3 day arrival) to 28 states: AL, AR, CO, GA, IA, IL, IN, KS, KY, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, ND, NE, NJ, NY, OH, OK, PA, SD, TN, TX, VA, VT, WI, WV; other continental US states by arrangement. $100 minimum for shipped orders; shipping $16.95-$35 by zone under $225, free at $225+. Farm pickup by appointment (no minimum).
