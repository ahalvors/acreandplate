About

Fourth-generation family ranch in Thomas, Oklahoma, founded in 1902 by Pierre Didier. Premium USDA Choice Black Angus beef, born, raised, and finished on one ranch. Grass-fed in their pastures and grain-finished on crops grown on the ranch. Dry-aged 21-28 days and shipped frozen nationwide.

Note: Family-owned since 1902 (4th generation, founded by Pierre Didier from South France). Cattle born, raised, and finished on one family ranch in Oklahoma—never outsourced. Dry-aged 21-28 days. Email: Didierranch1902@gmail.com; Mailing: P.O. Box 808, Thomas, OK 73669. No public phone listed.
