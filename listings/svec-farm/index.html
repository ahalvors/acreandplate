About

Fifth-generation family farm in Eastern South Dakota raising heritage breed Belted Galloway cattle on tallgrass prairie. 100% grassfed and grass-finished with no corn, soy, or grains. Cattle spend summers grazing mixed native grass prairies and winters fed hay raised on farm. USDA processed and sold frozen.

Note: Fifth-generation family farm. Pickup only at multiple SD locations - does not ship. USDA processed, CryoVac sealed, sold frozen.
