Together Farms

Mondovi, WI

Genetics

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef raised on the farm's own pastures with intensive rotational grazing (moved to new pasture daily); winter diet is stockpiled pasture and hay bought from local farms, with no corn silage, oats or grain; free-choice minerals and salt plus apple cider vinegar in the water; no added hormones, antibiotics or vaccines per the farm

About

Together Farms is a first-generation, pasture-based family farm in Mondovi, Wisconsin, raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef with intensive rotational grazing and no grain ever. Beef is processed at an Animal Welfare Approved butcher, sold through the on-farm store, local delivery and Midwest shipping, and served at the farm's seasonal Burger Nights.

Note: Farm address W93 Norden Rd, Mondovi, WI 54755. The farm says it raises animals organically but shows no organic certification, so none is claimed; the Animal Welfare Approved label refers to the butcher, not a farm certification. Breed not stated. Also sells lamb, pastured pork and other local foods. Beef bundles on the site run from $69 to $599 (Premium 1/8 Beef Bundle) on Nov 9, 2026. Burger Night runs about May to early October. Checked 2026-11-09.

Shipping

Local delivery Mondays (Menomonie area Wednesdays) to parts of Eau Claire, Chippewa, Trempealeau and Dunn counties ($39 minimum; $15, free over $199). 1-day Spee-Dee/UPS shipping to the rest of Wisconsin and select nearby states such as IA, IL and MN ($59 minimum; $24.99 under $199). 2-day UPS to states including IN, KS, KY, MI, MO, MT, NE, ND, OH and SD ($99 minimum; $49.99 under $249, free over $249). Pickup at the on-farm store in Mondovi.
