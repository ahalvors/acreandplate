John Henry's Meats

Millington, MI

Genetics

Grass-fed and grass-finished beef from a family farm in Millington, Michigan, selling since 2007; per the farm, cattle are 100% grass-fed, grazing pasture when available and eating hay and fermented silage in winter, with no antibiotics or hormones

About

John Henry's Meats is a family-owned farm in Millington, in Michigan's Thumb, that began selling its grass-fed beef at farmers' markets in 2007. It raises grass-fed, grass-finished beef alongside pastured pork and poultry, hand-cuts orders with custom cuts welcome, and ships frozen across much of the U.S. from its Millington storefront.

Note: Public prices seen 2026-11-16: boneless ribeye $37.49 (14 oz), NY strip $23.49 (10 oz), filet mignon $13.99 (4 oz), ground chuck $12.49/lb, 10 lb bulk ground beef & heart $125.99, quarter beef bundle $1,272.93 (about 175 lb hanging at $7.27/lb, about 88 lb take-home), half beef $2,670.48 (about 170 lb take-home), whole beef $5,267.99. Cattle breed is not stated and no certifications are claimed. The store also carries items such as wild-caught seafood and dairy; only the farm's own beef is the basis of this listing. Eggs sold at the Royal Oak Farmers' Market. Checked 2026-11-16.

Products Available

  • steaks: boneless ribeye, bone-in ribeye, New York strip, filet mignon, top sirloin, plus tenderloin and prime rib
  • ground beef blends (ground chuck 85/15, ground round 90/10, ground sirloin 93/7, beef & heart, beef & liver), patties and sliders, plus 10 lb bulk grinds
  • roasts: chuck, English, rump, sirloin tip, eye of round; brisket, short ribs, shanks and organ meats
  • quarter, half and whole beef bundles (priced by hanging weight)
  • steak and variety boxes, subscription boxes and a Premium Farmshare Membership

Shipping

Ships frozen via UPS to its 1-, 2- and 3-day zones from Michigan (not Alaska or Hawaii; the farm says some West Coast and rural addresses may be outside its zones, so check first). Shipping is tiered by order size, e.g. $9.99-$19.99 in Michigan and Ohio under $400; a flat $49.99 applies to CA, OR, ID, UT, NV, AZ, WY and WA. Local delivery routes in the area, and free pickup at The Grazing Table storefront, 4509 Park Dr, Millington, MI (Mon-Thu 9am-3pm, Fri 9am-noon).
