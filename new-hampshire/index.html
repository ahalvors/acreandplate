Yankee Farmer's Market (Pitcher Mountain Farm)

Warner, NH
Heritagescottish-highland

Scottish Highland cattle raised 100% grass-fed and grass-finished on open pasture at Pitcher Mountain Farm, with no hormones or antibiotics per the farm; some beef comes from partner local farms

Products: steaks (ribeye, bone-in ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, tenderloin), steak tips, kabobs, shaved steak and stew meat, ground beef and burgers…

Shipping: Ships frozen by UPS anywhere in the continental U.S. (orders go out Monday to Wednesday and arrive in 2 days or less); nationwide shipping on most bulk packages. Also pickup at the farm store, 360 Route 103 E, Warner

Phone 603-456-2833, info@yankeefarmersmarket.com. The farm says it also partners with trusted local farms for more selection, so not every beef cut is necessarily from its own Highland herd. Grass-finished and no-hormones/antibiotics claims are the farm's own. Farm store open Monday to Thursday 10 to 5 and Saturday 9 to 4.
