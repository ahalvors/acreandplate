Crossroads Beef

Carroll, IA
angus

Red Angus herd raised on pasture its entire life; 100% grass-fed and grass-finished with adaptive rotational grazing onto new pasture daily, a planted mix of perennial grasses and legumes, and minerals plus baled grasses in winter. Never in a feedlot, no growth hormones, no GMO feed and no mRNA vaccines per the farm

Products: ribeye, NY strip, tenderloin filet, top sirloin, sirloin tip, chuck eye and hanger steaks, chuck blade roasts and short ribs, single-source ground beef, burger patties, skinless beef brats and all-beef hot dogs…

Shipping: Ships frozen with dry ice on Mondays or Tuesdays (1-3 day arrival) to 28 states: AL, AR, CO, GA, IA, IL, IN, KS, KY, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, ND, NE, NJ, NY, OH, OK, PA, SD, TN, TX, VA, VT, WI, WV; other continental US states by arrangement. $100 minimum for shipped orders; shipping $16.95-$35 by zone under $225, free at $225+. Farm pickup by appointment (no minimum).

Contact is through the farm's website contact form only; no public phone or email is listed. The farm says it follows organic practices but is not USDA-certified organic, so no organic claim is made here. Beef is processed at a USDA-inspected facility; cuts are mostly wrapped in plastic and butcher paper (some vacuum-sealed). Public prices seen 2026-11-13 include ribeye from $24.87, NY strip from $20.17, ground beef from $12 and the Family Essentials 10 lb collection from $133.31. Beef share pricing is set per order.
