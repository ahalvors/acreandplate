K5 Wagyu

Doyle, TN
Wagyu

Registered Fullblood Black Wagyu (the ranch says 100% Wagyu genetics, registered and traceable to Japanese bloodlines) and F1 Wagyu (the ranch describes F1 as a first-generation cross of a purebred Wagyu sire and a high-quality Angus or other British breed dam). Cattle graze Tennessee pasture. Finishing method is not stated.

Products: steaks (filet mignon, ribeye, Delmonico, NY strip, picanha, Denver), ribs and roasts (center cut ribs, chuck ribs, picanha roast), ground Wagyu, fajita strips, shaved steak, kabobs, primal blend, burger patties…

Shipping: Local pickup at the farm in Middle Tennessee, or delivery within Tennessee. The ranch says it does not ship outside Tennessee at this time. Orders are vacuum-sealed, frozen, and labeled by cut and weight. No online cart; order by call, text, or inquiry.

Checked 2026-11-26 from kolstercattle.com (home, The Ranch, Wagyu Guide, How to Order, Contact, and the Wagyu Selection / menu page). Also known as Kolster Cattle Company.
