Emmett, ID

Regenerative family farm raising Freedom Ranger broilers moved to fresh pasture 7 days a week, plus laying hens raised with Saint John's Organic Farm in Emmett. No medications, vaccines, hormones, or antibiotics; non-GMO, corn-free, soy-free feed. Chickens processed at a local USDA-inspected facility.

Products:

  • Whole pasture chicken, listed at $7/lb (10% off 40 lb+)

How to buy: Pickup in Emmett or in Meridian at The Karlfeldt Center. Friday delivery in the Treasure Valley for $20, free over $250. No shipping listed.
