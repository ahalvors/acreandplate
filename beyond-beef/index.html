Grandad Farms

Emmett, ID
Pasture chickenPasture eggsAnimal feed

Regenerative family farm raising Freedom Ranger broilers moved to fresh pasture 7 days a week, plus laying hens raised with Saint John's Organic Farm in Emmett. No medications, vaccines, hormones, or antibiotics; non-GMO, corn-free, soy-free feed. Chickens processed at a local USDA-inspected facility.

Products:

  • Whole pasture chicken, listed at $7/lb (10% off 40 lb+)
  • Pasture eggs, unwashed and ungraded, 1 dozen every 2 weeks for $9 (subscription; the farm's site labels them organic, no certification listed)
  • Non-GMO, corn-free, soy-free poultry and pig feed, oyster shells, alfalfa pellets

How to buy: Pickup in Emmett or in Meridian at The Karlfeldt Center. Friday delivery in the Treasure Valley for $20, free over $250. No shipping listed.

Contact: Mark Ellison and family · (208) 207-4292 · mark@grandadfarms.com

FAFO Farms TX

Purmela, TX
Pasture eggsRaw dairyGoat milk soap

Central Texas farm in Purmela (Coryell County), founded in 2023 and run on Joel Salatin's model. Pasture-raised and rotationally grazed with mob grazing; the farm says its animals are soy-free, GMO-free, and not vaccinated. Sells eggs from free-range, corn- and soy-free chickens, plus raw milk, raw cream, butter, and goat milk soap. No beef for sale was listed. Also on X as @FAFOFarmsTX.

Products:

  • Regenerative raised eggs, corn- and soy-free, $8/dozen (listed as members only; out of stock when checked)
  • 2% raw cow's milk, 1 gallon, $10, farm share only (out of stock when checked)
  • Cream-on-top raw cow's milk, 1 gallon, $16, farm share only (out of stock when checked)
  • Raw cream, 16 oz, $10, farm share only (out of stock when checked)
  • Raw butter, 1 lb, $20, farm share only (out of stock when checked)
  • Goat milk soap, generally $10 per bar as listed on the soaps page (one citrus bar on sale from $14; some scents out of stock or on pre-order)

How to buy: Farm share and farm pickup at 1140 County Road 106, Purmela, TX 76566. Dairy is farm-share only; the farm says the share is currently at max capacity and keeps a waitlist (email TeamRarey@proton.me). Texas allows raw milk sales only on the farm, so dairy is farm pickup only. The contact page lists visit hours Monday–Friday 9am–5pm and Saturday 10am–5pm. No shipping listed.

Contact: (512) 221-3245 · TeamRarey@proton.me
