Rivas Ranch

Tobaccoville, NC

Breed not stated. The ranch says its beef is grass-fed and grass-finished on its North Carolina ranch, with no grain lot at the end. Every share comes from one animal and is never co-mingled, processed one animal at a time at a local, family-owned USDA-inspected plant. Halal processing is available on request

Products: quarter cow, half cow, and whole cow by the pound of hanging weight, Set-Price Box (quarter or half; Family, Grill, or Rancher + Chef), Shipped Quarter box…

Shipping: Only the Shipped Quarter ships: 70 lb frozen to the lower 48 and DC, shipping included. By-the-pound shares are pickup or delivery, not shipped. Free door delivery anywhere in North Carolina on a monthly run (third week of the month). Free pickup at the farm in Tobaccoville by appointment (5-7 PM daily), at the Greensboro Farmers Curb Market, or at a Ranch Route stop. The Ranch Route also stops in parts of Virginia, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New York, South Carolina, and Georgia.

Checked 2026-11-21 on rivasranch.com, https://rivasranch.com/pages/shop-beef, and https://rivasranch.com/pages/contact. Beef by the pound of hanging weight: quarter $9.25/lb (about 150-175 lb hanging, about $1,390-$1,620, $500 deposit), half $8.75/lb (about 300-325 lb hanging, about $2,630-$2,840, $950 deposit), whole $8.25/lb (about 600-650 lb hanging, about $4,950-$5,360, $1,200 deposit). Deposits are not refundable and come off the final invoice. The balance is invoiced from the processor's hanging weight, plus a 2% NC food tax, and is due within 3 days. Take-home weight is about 55-65% of hanging weight (a quarter is about 80-115 lb packaged). Shipped Quarter: 70 lb for $1,500 with shipping included to the lower 48 and DC; shipping is not refundable. Ground beef boxes: 10 lb $90 and 40 lb $340. The homepage lists freezer space as about 5 cu ft for a quarter, 10 for a half, and 20 for a whole; the shop-beef page says about 3-4 cu ft for a by-the-pound quarter, about 3 cu ft for a Set-Price quarter, about 8-10 for a half, and a full-size chest freezer or two for a whole. The homepage says Greensboro Curb Market; the shop-beef page says Greensboro Farmers Curb Market. Pork also ships frozen, with shipping billed when it ships.
