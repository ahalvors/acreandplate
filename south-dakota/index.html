Dakota Bluffs

Platte, SD

Breed not stated. The ranch says its cattle are 100% grass-finished and never grain-fed: on pasture all summer, fed hay over winter, then back on spring grass where they are finished. The ranch describes regenerative grazing, low-stress handling, no feedlots, no unnecessary antibiotics, and no synthetic hormones

Products: bulk beef shares by hanging weight: eighth and quarter $9.25/lb, half and whole $9/lb (deposits $100/$200/$300/$600; about 40% ground, the rest steaks, roasts, and ribs; dry-aged at least 10-14 days), Pure Pasture ground beef bundles in 1 lb packs: 10 lb $110 (+$19.99 shipping), 20 lb $215, 45 lb $475, 90 lb $950, Taste of the Prairie 20 lb sampler box $380 (first-order Welcome Pack $317)…

Shipping: Ships frozen anywhere in the lower 48 (not Alaska or Hawaii). Shares and most bundles ship free; the 10 lb ground bundle adds $19.99. Whole-beef buyers who pick up at the ranch save $1,000. Shares are harvested in the fall (usually September to November) and fulfilled in the fall

Checked 2026-11-20 on dakotabluffs.com (home, about, contact, product pages). The bulk share product pages say 'Deposits opening soon!', so share deposits may not be open yet. Share pricing is per lb of hanging weight, calculated at delivery: eighth about 100-200 lb hanging and quarter about 200-300 lb at $9.25/lb; half about 300-400 lb and whole about 700-800 lb at $9/lb; the final balance is invoiced before shipping. Packaged meat is typically about 60% of hanging weight. Offers a full refund if you don't like the beef ('Farmer's Handshake'). Processor is described only as a family-owned USDA processor, not named. No phone or email is shown on the ranch's own site (third-party directories list some, not used here). Breed and certifications not stated.
