Diamond D Ranch

Ramer, AL

Every animal born and raised on the family ranch in Ramer; grass-fed, grain-finished: raised on unsprayed pasture their whole lives with free-choice access to non-GMO grain after weaning while still on grass, organic hay in fall and winter

Products: individual cuts (ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, filet mignon, skirt and flank steak, brisket, short ribs, roasts, stew meat), 80/20 ground beef and burger patties, organs and marrow bones…

Shipping: Ships frozen (dry ice) to the 48 contiguous states at a flat rate by delivery zone shown at checkout; beef ships Monday-Wednesday, most orders leave the ranch within a week; no Alaska or Hawaii. Free pickup of online orders at the Montgomery Curb Market (1004 Madison Ave, Montgomery) Saturdays 9 am-12 pm, plus walk-up sales at the stall

Not certified organic (the ranch says it follows organic principles on unsprayed pasture). Grain-finished, not grass-finished. Breed not named on the ranch's website. Quarter, half and whole shares showed sold out on Oct 20, 2026 (1/8 share available). Email: theranchers@diamonddranch.online.
