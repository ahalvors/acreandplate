Diamond D Ranch

Ramer, AL

Every animal born and raised on the family ranch in Ramer; grass-fed, grain-finished: raised on unsprayed pasture their whole lives with free-choice access to non-GMO grain after weaning while still on grass, organic hay in fall and winter

Products: individual cuts (ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, filet mignon, skirt and flank steak, brisket, short ribs, roasts, stew meat), 80/20 ground beef and burger patties, organs and marrow bones…

Shipping: Ships frozen (dry ice) to the 48 contiguous states at a flat rate by delivery zone shown at checkout; beef ships Monday-Wednesday, most orders leave the ranch within a week; no Alaska or Hawaii. Free pickup of online orders at the Montgomery Curb Market (1004 Madison Ave, Montgomery) Saturdays 9 am-12 pm, plus walk-up sales at the stall

Not certified organic (the ranch says it follows organic principles on unsprayed pasture). Grain-finished, not grass-finished. Breed not named on the ranch's website. Quarter, half and whole shares showed sold out on Oct 20, 2026 (1/8 share available). Email: theranchers@diamonddranch.online.

Purely Pastured Farm

Foley, AL

Breed not stated. The farm says the beef is 100% grass-fed and grass-finished, rotationally grazed, and never given antibiotics, hormones, mRNA, or other vaccinations

Products: steaks, roasts, ground…

Shipping: No shipping. Farm pickup by appointment in Baldwin County plus select local pickup locations; home delivery within a 22-mile radius of the farm for Subscribe & Save customers ($200 minimum, first Monday monthly). Serves South Alabama and Northwest Florida (Mobile, Pensacola, Fairhope, Daphne, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach area). Shares are farm pickup.

Public prices seen 2026-11-23: cuts sold by the pound, including filet steak (2") $46.50/lb, chuck eye steak $19/lb, picanha $18/lb, chuck roast $13.25/lb, and brisket $12.75/lb. Steak & Burger Box small (6-8 lb) $125 (regular $150), large (12-14 lb) $229 (regular $250). Quarter, half, and whole shares are reserved by deposit for January processing. A 1/4 share is a $375 deposit at $7.75/lb hanging weight (the farm estimates about 192 lb hanging, about 130-145 lb take-home, about $1,488 for the beef plus about $225 processing paid to the processor). Half and whole deposit amounts are not stated. A 3.5% fee applies to credit cards. Address: 14190 Joe Norris Lane, Foley, AL 36535, Baldwin County. Phone 251-233-9407. Email purelypasturedfarm@gmail.com. Breed is not stated. No certifications are stated. No shipping: farm pickup by appointment in Baldwin County and at select local pickup locations (those locations are not named); home delivery within a 22-mile radius is for Subscribe & Save customers only ($200 minimum, first Monday of the month). Shares are farm pickup. The farm says it serves South Alabama and Northwest Florida (Mobile, Pensacola, Fairhope, Daphne, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach area). Checked 2026-11-23.
