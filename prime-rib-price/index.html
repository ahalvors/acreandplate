Source: USDA AMS Market News, Weekly Grocery Store Beef Feature Activity · report dated Nov 20, 2026 · ads running Nov 14, 2026 – Nov 26, 2026.

  • A year ago$12.84/lb+$0.11 · 9,494 stores
  • Last week$9.87/lb+$3.08 · only 4,363 stores advertised it
  • This week$12.95/lb11,266 stores advertised it

This is the average advertised price for conventional, fresh, regular-priced bone-in ribeye (rib) roasts at grocery stores USDA surveys, weighted by the number of stores running the ad. Bone-in ribeye roasts averaged $12.95 per pound compared to $12.84 per pound in 2025. Last week's $9.87 came from far fewer advertising stores (4,363 vs 11,266), so part of the week-over-week jump reflects which stores were running ads, not only price changes.

Wholesale reference

What the rib costs before it reaches the case

Wholesale, not retail. These are negotiated packer prices, FOB plant, for an uncut bone-in rib (lip-on ribeye). They are not what you pay at a counter, and the store still trims, cuts and packages the roast. Advertised grocery prices are promotional, which is why they can sit below the wholesale box price.
