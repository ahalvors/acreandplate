Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Who we are

Acre & Plate (acreandplate.com) is an independent directory of ranches that sell beef direct to consumers. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@acreandplate.com.

Browsing the site

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Like most websites, our host, Netlify, processes standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested in order to serve and secure the site.

Links to ranch websites include tracking parameters (such as utm_source=acreandplate) so ranches can see that a visit came from Acre & Plate. These parameters do not contain personal information about you.

When you contact us

If you email us, for example to update a ranch listing or ask a question, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it to reply and to keep the directory accurate. We do not sell your personal information.

Featured Ranch purchases

Featured Ranch placements are paid through Stripe. Stripe collects the payment details needed to complete the purchase and shares the order details with us; we do not see or store full card numbers. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

Shop and other sites

Our merchandise shop at acreandplate.printful.me is run on Printful, and ranch websites we link to are run by the ranches. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Retention and your rights

We keep emails and purchase records only as long as we need them for the purposes above or as required by law. You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@acreandplate.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
