Alaska Natural Foods (Blue Creek Livestock)

Delta Junction, AK
angus

Angus and Simmental lines being bred up with Murray Grey (an Australian Angus-Shorthorn cross the ranch picked for Delta Junction's cold). All cattle born, raised and finished on the ranch. Beef shares are grass-fed and barley-finished, with sprouted-barley fodder, ranch hay and local chopped barley in winter; 100% grass-fed beef is available on request in limited amounts (per the ranch)

Products: quarter, half and whole beef shares at $11.00 per lb hanging weight ($250 deposit), Cowboy's Choice Box and Premium Butcher Cuts box (1/16 beef share, 32-34 lb, $544), Alaska Rugged Ground Box (1/16 to 1/4 share of ground beef, $352-$1,376)…

Shipping: No shipping. Boxes are delivered to North Pole, Fairbanks, Palmer and Anchorage (the ranch calls or emails with a delivery date and time); beef share pricing covers delivery from Homer and Anchorage to Fairbanks and surrounding communities. The farm store in Delta Junction is temporarily closed

Phone (208) 390-5904 per the ranch's contact page; no public email (contact form). Beef shares are grass-fed and barley-finished per the product page, though the homepage says grass fed and finished; 100% grass-fed is by request only. Regenerative soil practices are the ranch's own description; no certifications claimed. Processor not named. Prices from the ranch's shop on Oct 30, 2026 and may change.
