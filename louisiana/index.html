Brookshire Farm

Abbeville, LA

Calves born on the farm and raised at their mothers' sides; 100% grass-fed and pasture-finished on native and selected cultivated forages, never supplemented with grain; cows selected for calves that grass-finish well and have easy-going dispositions

Products: steaks (ribeye, NY strip, filet mignon, sirloin, hanger, skirt, flank, bavette), roasts (rib, ribeye, tenderloin, chuck, rump), ground beef and stew meat…

Shipping: No shipping shown on the farm's site. Order online, then pick up on a scheduled date at the farm in Abbeville or at a pickup location near you (bring an ice chest); the site also offers home delivery in some ZIP codes
