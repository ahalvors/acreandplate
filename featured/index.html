How It Works

Who should get featured?

Ranches raising cattle with traceable bloodlines—Fullblood Wagyu, Akaushi, Japanese Black, heritage breeds. If you're already listed on Acre & Plate, Featured Ranch gives you premium visibility.

What's the commitment?

Monthly subscription. Cancel anytime. Your free directory listing stays active regardless of Featured Ranch status.

How do I track results?

We provide monthly traffic reports showing visits to your listing and clicks to your ranch website. All outbound links include UTM tracking so you can see Acre & Plate referrals in your own analytics.

What if my ranch isn't listed yet?

Visit our About page or check GitHub for instructions on adding your ranch to the directory. Featured Ranch placement is available once your listing is live.

Contact us at ranches@acreandplate.com for questions about featured placement, listing updates, or custom packages.
