Etta Hills Farm

Etta, MS

Breed not stated. The farm says the beef is 100% grass-finished, on pasture from birth through the last day, with rotational grazing and no hormones, antibiotics, or grain finishing. The farm says every protein is sent to an independent lab for phytonutrient-density testing

Products: steaks, roasts, ground…

Shipping: Farm pickup by appointment and free local delivery near Etta (Tue/Fri routes); continental-US frozen shipping (free over $399) is described on the site but marked 'coming soon' — confirm with the farm.

Public prices seen 2026-11-22: ribeye steak $34, NY strip $30, ground beef $12 (1 lb), flat iron $11, beef patties $15.50, brisket $145. Beef shares are fixed total prices, reserved by a deposit online with the balance due before pickup or delivery: quarter share $1,495 (about 100-120 lb packaged, standardized, not customizable), half share $2,895 (about 200-240 lb, custom cut), whole $5,595 (about 400-480 lb, custom cut). The deposit amount is not stated. Address: 90 Co Rd 253, Etta, MS 38627. Hours: Mon-Fri 9-4, Sat 8-1. Phone (662) 351-3882. Email chris@ettahillsfarm.com; hello@ettahillsfarm.com also appears on product pages. The farm says bare soil is down from 40%+ to under 5% and that 160+ plant species have been reintroduced; those are the farm's figures. Breed is not stated. No certifications are stated. Farm pickup is by appointment, and local delivery near Etta is free on Tuesday and Friday routes. Product pages say free shipping over $399, and the shipping page describes frozen dry-ice FedEx shipping to the continental US (not AK/HI), but the same shipping page also says 'Shipping Coming Soon' — confirm with the farm.
