Aquidneck Farms

Portsmouth, RI

100% grass-fed and grass-finished beef, never finished on grain; cattle are born on the farm, raised on mother's milk, naturally weaned and rotationally grazed, with farm-grown hay and silage through the winter. No hormones or prophylactic antibiotics in feed, and pastures are fertilized with the farm's own manure and compost (per the farm). The herd is selected for genetics suited to a grass-based system; the breed is not named

Products: steaks (NY strip, sirloin, rib steak, tenderloin, flat iron, hanger, skirt, flank, teres major, steak tips), ground beef, patties and meatball mix, roasts (chuck, rib, eye of round, top and bottom round, London broil), brisket, short ribs and stew beef…

Shipping: No shipping shown. Farm store at 333 Wapping Road, Portsmouth (Mon-Sat 11-5) with curbside pickup, plus local delivery on Thursdays and Fridays through the online store (fee based on location); the cuts page says orders over $350 are delivered

Phone (401) 849-0337, info@aquidneckfarms.com. Grass-finished and no-hormones claims are the farm's own; no organic or third-party certification is claimed. The farm notes its management plan is NRCS-approved and reviewed by Rhode Island DEM. Online store prices weren't readable tonight, so no prices are listed.
