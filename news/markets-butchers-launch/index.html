🏪 New Markets & Butchers hub launched

September 12, 2026

We've added a new Local Activity section tracking farmers markets and butcher shops where Bay Area residents buy ranch-direct beef. Three farmers markets are now listed including Pleasanton's Saturday market where Engler Beef from Sonora sells plus 19 butcher shops across Bay Area counties.

This hub complements the ranch directory: ranchers use farmers markets to connect with local communities, and butcher shops give residents another way to find quality beef beyond online orders. Vendor lineups change frequently, so we note that customers should always confirm current offerings at the market or shop.

Starting with Bay Area coverage, but expanding nationwide. Know a farmers market where local ranchers sell traceable beef? Let us know.
