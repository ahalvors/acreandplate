📸 Visiting Rosewood Ranches, Texas Wagyu country on the Trinity River

October 12, 2026

Acre & Plate visited Rosewood Ranches in Ennis, Texas, an American Wagyu operation along the Trinity River. We saw black cattle out on wide, open grassland.

According to Rosewood, the ranch covers more than 10,000 acres along the Trinity River, and its stewardship goes back to the Sands family in 1857. The ranch says it crosses Wagyu genetics with Black Angus lineage, with cattle raised and finished on pasture.

The ranch is owned by a subsidiary of the Dallas-based Rosewood Corporation, which is owned by the Caroline Hunt Trust Estate, according to the company history.

Why it isn’t in the directory: Rosewood sells its beef to chefs and through distributors such as Allen Brothers, which describes it as pasture-raised with no added hormones. The ranch doesn’t offer direct-to-consumer checkout for fresh beef, so it doesn’t meet our ranch-direct rule and isn’t listed.
