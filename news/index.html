New Markets & Butchers hub launched

We've added a new Local Activity section tracking farmers markets and butcher shops where Bay Area residents buy ranch-direct beef. Three farmers markets are now listed including Pleasanton's Saturday market where Engler Beef from Sonora sells plus 19 butcher shops across Bay Area counties.

This hub complements the ranch directory: ranchers use farmers markets to connect with local communities, and butcher shops give residents another way to find quality beef beyond online orders. Vendor lineups change frequently, so we note that customers should always confirm current offerings at the market or shop.

Starting with Bay Area coverage, but expanding nationwide. Know a farmers market where local ranchers sell traceable beef? Let us know.

Thursday morning

Directory now lists 30 verified ranches

The Acre & Plate directory has grown to 30 ranches across the U.S. selling beef with traceable bloodlines: Fullblood Wagyu, Akaushi, and heritage breeds like Randall Lineback and Dexter. Each ranch is verified from public websites with documented genetics claims.

Recent additions include ranches from Wyoming to Florida, with most offering nationwide shipping. The directory filters by breed (17 Wagyu, 4 Akaushi, 2 heritage) and state, helping customers find ranch-direct beef from named herds.

Not grocery store Wagyu marketing. These are working ranches with DNA-tested or registered cattle, selling direct to consumers who care about sourcing.

Now tracking seasonal deals across ranches

A new Seasonal Deals section tracks verified sale, clearance, and overstock pricing from ranch websites. When a ranch marks down ribeyes, ground beef, or other cuts, we list it here with the verified price and direct link to purchase.

Current deals include discounts on Wagyu ground beef, ribeye packs, and bulk share pricing. All prices are verified from ranch websites as of the listed date. Customers should always confirm current availability before ordering.

This complements the main directory by highlighting time-sensitive opportunities to try ranch-direct beef at reduced pricing, especially for customers new to buying direct from ranches.

Bay Area ranch coverage expands

California ranches with local pickup or Bay Area delivery are now prominently featured in the directory. Several ranches offer weekend pickup at their properties or delivery to Bay Area customers, giving local residents an alternative to nationwide shipping.

For customers in Livermore, Pleasanton, and surrounding areas, this means access to Wagyu and heritage beef from ranches within driving distance plus the option to visit ranch properties and see where their beef comes from.

The directory notes shipping limitations clearly: Local pickup only or California shipping appears in each listing, so customers know what to expect before clicking through to ranch websites.
