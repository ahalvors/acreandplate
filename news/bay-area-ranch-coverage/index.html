🗺️ Bay Area ranch coverage expands

September 03, 2026

California ranches with local pickup or Bay Area delivery are now prominently featured in the directory. Several ranches offer weekend pickup at their properties or delivery to Bay Area customers, giving local residents an alternative to nationwide shipping.

For customers in Livermore, Pleasanton, and surrounding areas, this means access to Wagyu and heritage beef from ranches within driving distance plus the option to visit ranch properties and see where their beef comes from.

The directory notes shipping limitations clearly: Local pickup only or California shipping appears in each listing, so customers know what to expect before clicking through to ranch websites.
