🎄 New: Holiday Prime Rib Price Board, grocery avg $12.95/lb

November 26, 2026

Acre & Plate now has a live Prime Rib Price Board for the holidays. It shows the national grocery-ad average for a bone-in rib roast: $12.95/lb across 11,266 stores, with advertised prices from $6.99 to $20.99 per lb (a year ago: $12.84). Source: the USDA Agricultural Marketing Service weekly grocery beef feature report dated Nov 20, 2026, covering ads for Nov 14 to Nov 26.

Next to it: the USDA wholesale Choice 109E rib at $14.05/lb (Nov 25 report), and what 14 in-stock roasts from 11 ranches in the directory cost direct, $30.89 to $150 per lb as listed on each ranch's own site on Nov 26. The board also has a quick guide to how much prime rib to buy per guest.

Ranch-direct roasts usually ship frozen, and several ranches have last-ship dates before Christmas, so order early. See the board at acreandplate.com/prime-rib-price or browse holiday deals and all ranches. The board refreshes through Dec 31.
