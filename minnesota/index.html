Hidden Stream Farm

Elgin, MN

100% grass-fed beef: cattle live outdoors year round on biodiverse pasture in the warmer months and dry pasture and stored forages in winter, and never receive grain; no drugs, antibiotics, hormones or steroids per the farm, with regenerative, rotational grazing practices

Products: steaks, roasts, ground beef…

Shipping: Weekly home delivery to all lower 48 states: Rochester-area and Twin Cities delivery by the farm van, regional delivery via Spee-Dee (free over $99), and FedEx/UPS Ground or 2nd Day Air nationally (free over $199). Orders are due by Sunday midnight for delivery the following week. Also sold at farmers markets and area retail stores.

Farm address 27079 County Road 25, Elgin, MN 55932. Run by Eric and Lisa Klein and their six kids. The farm became certified organic in 2018 per its story page, but the beef pages do not carry an organic label, so no organic beef claim is made here. The farm also acts as a local food hub, reselling foods from partner farms in Minnesota and Wisconsin, and sells pork, chicken, lamb, Katy's Eggs and more. Breed not stated. Prices require a free store account. Checked 2026-11-10.
