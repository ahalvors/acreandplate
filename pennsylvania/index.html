Hershberger Heritage Farm (Berger Box)

Sellersville, PA

Grass-fed and grass-finished beef, rotationally grazed on pasture 100% of the time with no pesticides, herbicides, GMOs or artificial fertilizers, on a Bucks County family farm founded in 2014 per the farm

Products: steaks: ribeye, New York strip, filet mignon, sirloin, tri-tip and 'rare' cuts (flat iron, bavette, flank, picanha, skirt, Denver), 80/20 ground beef, burger patties, chipped steak and beef cubes, plus 10 lb and 13 lb ground beef boxes, roasts: chuck roast, London broil, brisket and short ribs…

Shipping: Ships frozen in insulated packaging to the Northeastern United States via USPS, UPS or FedEx; the site shows $15 shipping on all orders and a $165 minimum for custom boxes. In-person shopping and pickup at the farm store, 1614 Ridge Road, Sellersville, PA (Mon, Wed 9am-5pm; Fri-Sat 9am-6pm; Sun 10am-5pm; closed Tue/Thu).

Public prices seen 2026-11-15: ribeye from $49.50 (1.1 lb), NY strip from $38.70, ground beef $13/lb, 10 lb ground beef box $168.99 (no subscription), Farmer's Choice boxes $165 (regular) and $305 (large), 1/4 beef $1,590 (about 100 lb; the farm currently adds a free 5 cu ft chest freezer for a limited time). The farm's 2023 blog describes its livestock as Certified Organic; no certificate is shown on the site, so that is the farm's own claim and is not tagged here. Cattle breed is not stated. No public phone number; use the email or the site's contact form. Also sells pastured pork, pasture-raised chicken and eggs. Checked 2026-11-15.
